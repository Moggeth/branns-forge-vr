- Hold the pick's special to see exactly where the wall will go, then release to cast it.
- The wall now starts 1.6 m in front of you, so it never lifts you off your feet.
- An earthy crust surges forward at 10 m/s, cracks open and releases glowing blue crystal.
- The rising front throws enemies back for 65 damage and lifts your allies.
- Your end of the wall is a ramp you can walk up, and every section can be mined for stone.
- After 60 seconds the wall shatters into stone shards and sinks. You can keep two walls up at once.
- Added sound, plus rumble on controllers and phones.
