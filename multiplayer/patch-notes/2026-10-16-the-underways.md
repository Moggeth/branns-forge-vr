title: The Underways (experimental)
---
- An old mine has opened in the north mountain foot. Walk into its timbered portal to delve the **Glimmerdelve**, a dungeon built fresh from the world's seed.
- Every dungeon follows a plan: a quiet way in, a room that teaches you its danger, fights that build, treasure off the beaten path, a locked gate whose key is guarded somewhere nearby, a shrine to rest at, and a crowned guardian at the bottom.
- Rooms wake as you step into them. Skeletons crawl up out of the floor, golems stand guard, and loose roofs and firedamp vents keep a steady rhythm you can learn and use against your foes.
- Swing at pots and chests to break them open for Gold, Crystal, rare ore and the odd apple.
- Topple the guardian and its hoard bursts open: a fountain of Gold, Crystal and star ore, a share of glory for everyone inside and a piece of Quartermaster kit each. Then a ring of light opens the way home.
- Clear a dungeon and it shifts behind you: the next delve is somewhere new. Leave early through the ladder's circle of light and it waits for you as it was.
- Every room has a little story to find, from a lost miner's half-written letter to twelve tiny pickaxes and one very big empty hook.
> This is the first of three dungeon styles; a crypt and a sunken keep are on the way.
