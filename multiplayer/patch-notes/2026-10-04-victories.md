title: Wins you can see and hear
patch: 2026-10-04a
pr: 11
---
- Nine kinds of win that used to pass silently now celebrate with gem flecks, a blue ring or a pillar of light, a bell chime and a rumble. These include repelling a raid, salvage, surveys, recruits and deliveries.
- Loot you pick up flies to your belt.
- A won raid raises a gold and blue pillar at the forge and tells you what it banked, for example "Raid repelled! Your stores gain 4 iron, 5 wood, 2 crystal and 1 star ore."
