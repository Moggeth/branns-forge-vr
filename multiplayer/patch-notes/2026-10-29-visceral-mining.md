title: Mining that hits back
---
- **Every pick blow now lands with real weight.** The spike bites into the rock, holds for a heartbeat, then levers out with a twist that pops the chunk free. Each blow kicks a little harder than the last as the rock weakens.
- **The rock reacts.** It rocks back on its bed away from your swing and settles. Crystal, mithril and meteors glow a little brighter from inside the deeper you dig.
- **Ore looks like ore when you hit it.** Iron throws rusty grit and silvery flecks, gold scatters glittering yellow bits, crystal sheds violet shards, and so on, all with mineral-tinted sparks. A few fat chunks land in the grass and stay there for a moment before they sink away.
- **The last blow is a payoff.** Instead of vanishing, the rock bursts apart. Its pieces are flung out, tumble, bounce and settle under a cloud of dust and a spray of sparks, and your reward hops out of the rubble.
- **New mining sounds.** You hear a crisp steel tick and the ore's own ring (iron rings, crystal chimes like glass, gold clinks), then a crack that climbs in pitch as the rock gives. Breaking it brings a deep whump, rubble raining down and a rising chime.
- Phones buzz in time with the bite and the break.
- Fixed impact dust, which wasn't showing up for any material. Axe chops, hammer thuds and stone hits now all puff a little dust as they were meant to.
