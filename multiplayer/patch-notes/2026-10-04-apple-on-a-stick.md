title: Apples on a stick
pr: 37
---
- Shoot an apple out of an Orchard tree with an arrow or a Siegebird bolt and it falls out skewered on the shaft. It tumbles, bounces and settles where it lands.
- Walk up and press **E** (or tap it on a phone) to take it, then bite it off the stick: three bites, **50 health** in total, with crunchy bite marks you can see.
- A Timberline bolt that hits an apple bursts it into juice and chunks.
- Catch apples with the Timberline's wire and it slices them clean in half. Each half drops to the ground and can be picked up and eaten in two bites for **25 health**.
- Everyone sees the same apples. Picked apples grow back on the tree after about 3 minutes.
