title: Hero levels and weapon talents
headline: Heroes Rising

- Heroes now earn **experience** and **level up**, quickly. Two skeletons are enough for level 2, and you can reach level 10 for now.
  - XP comes from kills (skeleton 20, golem 60, ent 45, stumpling 8), felling trees (10) and breaking ore (6, or 15 for star ore).
  - **Every level gives +20% health, +20% weapon damage and one talent point.** A level 10 hero has 280 health and hits 2.8 times as hard.
  - Levelling up fills your health. A golden column of light, a fanfare and a big banner mark the moment, and everyone nearby sees your column.
- Your level badge and a gold XP bar sit beside your health. A glowing number on the badge means you have talent points to spend.
- Press **N** (or tap the level badge) to open the new **Talents** panel. Every weapon has two specialties, each costing one point, so you can favour the weapons you love. Resetting your talents is free, any time.
  - **Axe.** *Limbsplitter*: every swing that lands on an arm, leg or head lops it clean off. *Whirlwind*: your cleave becomes a full spin with 30% more reach and recharges in 4 seconds (was 6).
  - **Stonewhisper.** *Motherlode*: ore cracks one strike sooner and drops twice as much. *Geode Burst*: when your crystal wall ends, it bursts into shards that hurt and hurl every foe beside it.
  - **Siegebird.** *Golden Flock*: the golden songbird comes three times as often. *Skewer*: every bolt punches clean through up to three foes.
  - **Emberwing.** *Ember Volley*: a full draw looses three arrows in a fan. *Firestorm*: Phoenix Flare recharges in 5 seconds (was 10) and its blaze covers twice the ground.
  - **Emberdew.** *Firebomb*: a lit flask explodes where it lands, burning and throwing back every foe nearby. *Tar Pit*: spills are far bigger and slow every foe that wades through them.
  - **Wyrmspout.** *Wyrmflood* and *Clinging Fire* are listed now and arrive with the Wyrmspout's upcoming overhaul.
- Your level and talents are kept with the world under your hero's name, so they are waiting for you when you come back.
- The bone has left the hotbar. Wyrmspout is now **6** and Emberdew is **7**. You can still pick up a bone with Grab and swing or throw it.
