title: Apples, bite by bite, and hungry songbirds
---
- Eating an apple off a stick now visibly eats it down. After each bite it turns to show you what's gone, so you can see it's two-thirds eaten before the last bite.
- When you finish, the core slides off the stick and drops to the ground. A few songbirds swoop in, hop around it, peck it clean and fly away.
- Other players see your core and its birds too.
