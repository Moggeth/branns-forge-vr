- The winch now works like one real machine: the clutch bites with a clunk, two crank turns wind the drum, the pawl clicks over the ratchet, and you can see the cable wind on.
- The strings ride the bending arms, and on firing the arms overshoot, the string hums and the stock kicks.
- Songbirds stay after delivering a bolt. They perch in a nearby tree or fly off toward the mountains, and felling their tree sends them flying.
- New materials: oiled walnut, brushed brass, blued steel and waxed cord.
- **Perfect wind:** fire just as the latch clicks and your next reload takes 0.75 seconds instead of 1.0. A blue glint, a chime and a rumble confirm it. You get one try per reload.
- **VR:** grab the crank knob with your free hand and turn it to wind. Beat the songbird to the latch for the quick reload.
