- **You steer the dragon now.** It flies where you look, banking into turns, so you can sweep it across the whole battlefield instead of circling one spot. Stray more than about 36 m from where you called it and it swings back round on its own.
- **New camera.** The ride starts in a chase view behind and above the dragon so you can see the whole beast at work. Press **C** (or tap the camera button on the ride timer) to swap to the saddle view and back.
- **A bigger, grander dragon.** New body with huge, properly dragon-sized wings and deep, gusty wingbeats that kick up dust when you fly low and give the saddle a little buffet.
- **Fire Breath is real fire now.** Instead of a straight beam, the dragon spits blobs of flame that arc through the air under gravity and splash where they land, 4.5 damage plus a burn within about 2 m of each splash. Flames are hot white at the heart, licking orange at the edges, with sparks and smoke.
- **No more painting the map black.** Scorch marks are smaller and lighter and fade away after a few seconds.
- The ride still lasts **20 seconds**, and Fireballs and Comet Dive work as before.
