title: A more responsive welcome
---
- The turn-phone button responds while the world is still loading. Cancelling an early join now keeps you in the welcome screen.
- Loading is split into smaller steps. Optional scenery details can finish loading after you enter the world.
- Automatic graphics adjusts the 3D resolution gradually when the device is struggling, while keeping menus and controls sharp. Choose a fixed preference in Menu → Input → Graphics.
- Turning your phone keeps the same world and loaded assets. Fixed a viewport problem when rotating during loading.
- Nearby heroes' axes and picks take fewer drawing operations while retaining their original shapes and materials.
