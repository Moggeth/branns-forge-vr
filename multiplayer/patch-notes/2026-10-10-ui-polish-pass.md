title: Tidier, clearer menus and hotbar
---
- The dragon call button now has its own spot above the hotbar instead of sitting on top of your weapons. The weapon hint and supplies strip each get their own row too.
- The supplies strip only shows what you have, plus wood, stone and gems, instead of a long row of zeros.
- Solo worlds no longer show the Copy code, Copy invite and Join QR buttons, or the invite section in the World menu, since there is nobody to invite.
- The Quartermaster's shelves have more breathing room, wider cards that are easier to read, rarity-coloured picture frames, and items you can't afford yet stay readable while telling you how much more Gold you need.
- Fixed the Hero and God cards in the World menu squashing their names into "He / ro" and "Go / d".
- The God's Forge button now looks like a button.
- Phones: the Healing Draught chip no longer covers your health numbers, Heroes no longer see a mana row, and the Full screen button no longer covers the close button on the shop and Talents panels.
- Phones: the supplies strip moved to the top of the screen instead of hiding behind the weapon buttons, and the dragon call button is a tidier size.
