title: Turn for more adventure
---
- Opening the game on a phone held upright now shows a little animated phone tipping onto its side, with the world inside it widening to reveal more. Turn your phone and it gets out of the way.
- Prefer playing upright? Tap **Stay upright** and it won't ask again this visit.
- On Android, after you turn your phone sideways, your next tap takes the game full screen and keeps it sideways. You can also tap **Turn it for me** to do both at once.
  - iPhone browsers don't let web pages go full screen. Adding the game to your Home Screen opens it without the browser bars instead.
> Browsers only allow full screen after a tap, so turning the phone arms it and your next tap finishes the job.
