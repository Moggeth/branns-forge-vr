title: The marksman's rune
---
- When you fire a golden Siegebird bolt, a golden rune circle now snaps open at the eagle's beak and the bolt flies straight through its centre.
- The circle carries an eagle with spread wings, a ring of runes and a sharpshooter's scope marks. A smaller echo circle opens just ahead, and a ring of light rolls outward.
- It flashes for about a second, so it never blocks your next shot. Everyone nearby sees it too.
