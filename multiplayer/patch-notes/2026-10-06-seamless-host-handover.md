title: The world carries on when the host leaves
---
- When the player hosting your world leaves, the next player now takes over by themselves straight away. Nobody has to press **Continue world here** any more, and the world keeps its terrain, buildings, creatures and everyone's packs.
- Phones and headsets can now carry the world on too. Before, only a computer could take over, so a group of phone players was left waiting on a paused world.
- If the host closes the game or switches to another app, the world is handed over before they go, usually in well under a second.
- If the host's game crashes or loses signal, the world moves on in about 4 seconds (was 15 seconds or more, plus a button press).
- During a switch your game stays live: you keep your view, your controls and your mouse capture, and a little note says who is carrying the world now.
- Creatures no longer jump backwards when a new host picks up the world a moment behind. Time eases for a second or two until everything lines up.
> If the old host comes back, they simply rejoin as a player.
