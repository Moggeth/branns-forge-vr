title: Mist, sunbeams and moody light
---
- **Sunbeams through the trees.** Low sun now pours soft golden shafts down through the crowns, with dust drifting in them. On a clear night the moon does the same in faint silver.
- **Ground mist.** A low mist now drifts across the meadow, gathering in the valleys. It is thickest at dawn, lingers through dusk and night, and rolls in heavy with fog, rain and snow.
- **The sun glows through the haze.** Look toward a low sun and the air itself lights up warm around it.
- **Rain makes the world wet.** Rocks, paths and grass darken and shine, and puddles gleam on flat ground. They dry out slowly after the rain stops.
- **Snow settles.** During snowfall a white cover slowly builds on every upward-facing surface, then melts away once the snow ends. Glowing crystals and magic stay bare so they still shine through.
- **A lower sun all day** for long shadows and warmer light, and evenings that sink gently into dusk.
- On computers: light shafts break through gaps in the canopy, magic and crystals glow at night, and a soft colour grade and vignette pull it all together. Phones get the mist, sunbeams, rain and snow without the heavier screen effects.
> We wanted the world to feel like a place you could breathe in: misty mornings, golden evenings, wet stone after a storm.
