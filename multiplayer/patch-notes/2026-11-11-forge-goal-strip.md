date: 2026-11-11
area: Interface
title: Your next forge goal, always in sight
---
- **The supplies strip now shows what you're working toward.** In the Great Wilds it leads with the next weapon to forge, and each resource shows how much you have and how much you need, for example 2/6 Wood. Once a resource is covered it turns green.
- **When you can afford it, the goal glows forge-orange.** It pulses when you're standing at the forge, so you know it's time to forge.
- Once every weapon is forged, or in Brann's Valley, the goal becomes the next temper for the weapon in your hand.
- **Iron is now one of the three main resources on the strip, with Wood and Crystal.** It always shows, even at zero. Stone moves to the extras, which only show when you're carrying some.
- Iron ore you're carrying now counts as Iron when you temper, just as it does when you forge a weapon.
- The empty Healing Draught chip no longer shows in the Great Wilds, because there's no well there to fill it.
- Phones: the talent "+1" no longer covers the HP label, and the draught chip fits inside the vitals card when you hold your phone upright.
