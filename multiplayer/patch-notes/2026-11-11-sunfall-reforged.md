title: The Sunfall Sledge, reforged
---
- **A new sledge.** The Sunfall has been rebuilt from scratch. The plain black barrel is gone. The new head is a faceted billet of blackened iron with two shrink-fitted steel bands, a broad steel striking face and a smaller drift face behind. Each cheek carries a sunstone ringed by twelve gold rays. The ash haft is now riveted with steel straps and wrapped in oxblood leather.
- **It holds the heat of the sun.** The head was cast around a sunstone heart, and four vents on top let its heat out as light. The vents and cheek stones glow, and embers drift up out of the vents.
- **The slam spends that heat.** The vents and stones go almost dark, then fill back up as the slam recharges. The gold studs at the ends of the long rays light up one by one, clockwise, as a built-in charge meter. When the slam is ready again, the vents burst with sparks.
- **A real slam.** Pressing Special now heaves the sledge up over your shoulder and drives it into the ground. The blow lands when the face hits the earth, not when you press the key.
- **The ground answers.** Every slam, yours or anyone else's, now throws a white-gold flash, a sunburst shockwave that runs exactly as far as the slam reaches, and a star of glowing cracks that cool from gold to red to soot. Clods and stones bounce away, along with sparks and a ring of dust. It replaces the old earth-raise effect, which didn't match what a sledge does.
- On phones, the slam has the same cracks and shockwave without the extra light, so your frame rate stays where it was.
> The whole slam is drawn with one light and four small batches, and they are only drawn while a slam is playing.
