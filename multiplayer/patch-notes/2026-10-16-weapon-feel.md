title: Weapons hit like they mean it
---
- Every melee blow now reacts to what it strikes. An axe bites into a trunk, sticks for a heartbeat and is wrenched back out; steel on rock jars and bounces back with a ring in your hands; blades carry on through bone; the dirt takes a heavy chunk.
- Fast swings leave a soft smear in the air behind the head of your weapon.
- Hits and shots give your view a small, sharp kick that settles straight away. The crossbow, bow, Stormreel, Wyrmspout's Gout and thrown Emberdew each have their own.
- Impacts look like their materials: long pale splinters and bark from wood, a bright glint and a spray of sparks when steel meets stone, glassy shards from crystal and clods of earth from the ground.
- Your own hits now show and sound the instant they land, even in rooms hosted far away.
- Phones get a short buzz on each hit, shaped to what you struck.
