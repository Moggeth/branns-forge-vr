title: Sunthread, the Radiant staff
---
- Heroes have a new tool on **key 8**: **Sunthread**, a pearl-and-gold staff crowned with a spinning sun crystal.
- It's a real light source. Ground, trees, buildings and foes around you glow warm in its light, far more at night.
- Hold attack to cast a **sun-thread**: a rope of light that arcs from the crown, latches onto a nearby foe (or lands on the ground) and carries the light with it, so whatever you touch lights up.
- Press **Q** (or right-click) for **Radiant Nova**: a burst of sunlight that rings out across the ground around you. 5 second cooldown.
- On phones the staff's button reads **Shine**.
> This is a first look at the staff and its light. Healing friends and draining foes come next.
