title: The Armoury and the Flockwright
---
- **A new building: the Armoury.** A cheerful timber-and-stone hall stands in the meadow just outside Dawnhold's gate, front turned toward the road. Spears and spare crossbows line the walls, a fletcher's bench is busy with half-made bolts, a dovecote sits on the roof, and there's a practice target out front.
- **A new crossbow: the Flockwright.** It rests on a lantern-lit stand in the middle of the Armoury. Walk up and press **E** (or Collect) to take one down. Heroes from either side are welcome.
  - Once taken, it's yours on key **9**, and it stays yours between sessions.
  - It looses heavy bolts that hit more than twice as hard as the Siegebird's, but it fires slower.
  - Six goggled birds ride on it. They bob and peek while you wait, startle when it fires, then wind it and seat the next bolt themselves.
  - Right-click (or tap Aim) to raise the ring sight.
