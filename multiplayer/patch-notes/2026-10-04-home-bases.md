title: Dawnhold and Grumblekeep
---
- Two home bases now stand in opposite corners of every new world: **Dawnhold** in the south-west and **Grumblekeep** in the north-east. Pick your side from Menu → Home bases.
- Each base sits under a shield dome. Your own side walks straight through; the other side bounces off.
- Three shield pylons hold each dome up. Break all three and the dome drops for 90 seconds. Then you can smash the heart forge inside.
- Smash a heart to win the siege. The winners' stores gain 12 iron, 12 wood, 6 crystal and 3 star ore for each level of the fallen base. Three minutes later the base rebuilds one level stronger, up to level 5.
- Every 60 seconds each barracks sends a squad down the lane. Dawnhold fields stone-and-brass forgelings; Grumblekeep sends skeletons, and from level 2 a golem joins each squad.
- Two crystal spires (short range, fast zaps) and two ballistas (22 m range) guard each gate. They shoot enemy troops first and enemy heroes only when no troops are in range.
- A fountain inside each dome heals your side by 10 health a second while you stand inside your dome.
- Heroes on opposite sides can now hit each other. When you fall, you respawn at your own heart forge.
- The three-minute skeleton raid and the skeleton camps are retired in new worlds. Grumblekeep's squads take their place.
> Older saved worlds keep playing as they did before.
