title: Room to grow, and the new Growth Wave
---
- Trees from **Grove** and **Tree Ring** no longer grow into each other. Each tree now leaves room for its neighbours' crowns.
- Growth spells adapt to what is around them. A tree that would land on a rock, a building, a Hero or another tree grows on the nearest open ground instead.
  - Grove trees can shift up to 3.5 m, and Tree Ring trees up to 2.4 m.
  - A growth spell is refused only when not a single tree fits, and then it costs no mana. The preview says "No room to grow here" first.
- The preview now shows exactly where each tree will grow.
- New spell: **Growth Wave** (**H**, 30 mana). Drag out a band up to 18 m long and 6 m wide, and a wave of growth rolls along it, raising about 8 trees one after another.
  - A tap grows a 12 m band across your view.
  - It drops healing apples like Grove and adds 3 Bloom.
- Trees from older worlds keep their places.
- Ents that take root after walking also keep the same spacing.
