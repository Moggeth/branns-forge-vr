title: Three new kinds of tree
---
- **Highland Fir.** Tall, straight spires of layered, drooping boughs with bright new growth on every tip and cones hanging underneath. They climb the rim of Brann's Valley and fill the pine slopes and tundra of the Great Wilds.
- **Emberleaf Maple.** A big rounded crown of ember orange, scarlet and gold on smooth grey bark, with winged seeds tucked under the leaves. Look for them in the woods and among the oaks.
- **Dawnbloom Cherry.** A low, spreading umbrella of pink blossom on dark banded bark. They brighten the open meadows and the village edge.
- Every new tree chops, notches, falls, splits into logs and leaves a stump exactly like the old ones, and Ents and Stumplings can wake from them too.
- Brann's Valley keeps all of its apple trees where they were. Some of its oaks and birches have been replaced by the new trees.
- In the Great Wilds each biome now has its own mix of the six trees, so pine forests finally look like pine forests.
