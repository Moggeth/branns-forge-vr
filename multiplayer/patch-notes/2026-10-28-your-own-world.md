title: Your own world comes first
---
- **Play now takes you to your own world.** It's just you, on your device, and no room needed. The first time, a fresh Great Wilds is lit for you. After that, Play picks up right where you left off.
- **Play with friends** sits just under it. It still finds the busiest world near you, or lights a new one. Invite links still take you straight to your friend's world.
- **The Great Wilds is now the recommended world** in New world… and has lost its "experimental" tag. Every new world starts as your own unless you tick **Open to friends**.
- **The keeps of Brann's Valley are at peace.** Dawnhold and Grumblekeep still stand, and Grumblekeep still guards its walls if you go raiding. Ring the barracks bell and a squad will march out with you. If you miss the lane battles, the host can start the keep war from the Menu and make peace again any time. Older saved worlds come back peaceful too.
