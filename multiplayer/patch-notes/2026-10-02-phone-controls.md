title: Play on your phone
---
- Phones and tablets get two thumb sticks.
  - Heroes get Move, Look, Attack and Jump.
  - Gods get Pan, Orbit and Tilt, pinch to zoom, then tap the ground to aim and press Cast.
- Works in portrait and landscape, with a left-handed stick swap.
- Added a full-screen button, and the game can be added to your home screen.
