- **Emberdew is an old miner's lamp now.** Long before anyone thought of throwing them, Brann's miners filled these squat cut-glass lamps with ember dew to light the deep tunnels. Each one shows it: a dented brass foot, a lantern guard with its carry handle folded back, a brass burner with a little wheel for raising the wick, soot up the shoulders, the miners' crossed pick and hammer stamped on the front, and a tin tag scratched with the shifts it worked. The wick sits dead centre, and that's what you light. The fiery dew inside sloshes as you walk, turn and throw, and you'll see tiny embers drifting up through it.
- **Hold right-click to light the wick and aim.** You raise the bottle to your shoulder and a thin arc shows exactly where it will land. Left-click while aiming to hurl it lit, more than twice as far as before (13 m/s, was 6).
- **A plain left-click still tosses an unlit puddle of fuel** that you can light later with Wyrmspout.
- **Bottles smash for real now.** Glass shards skitter across the ground, the dew splashes up in a crown, and a lit bottle goes off with a soft orange whump. A thrown bottle tumbles end over end on the way.
- **New Q: Cinder Cluster.** Hold Q to see the arc and a little ring where each bottle will land, then let go to lob a big jug high into the air (aim higher to throw it further). At the top it pops and rains five little lit bottles in a ring, and each one smashes into its own burning puddle. It recharges in 12 seconds, and both Emberdew talents work on every bottle.
- **On phones and in VR,** Attack always throws a lit bottle along the arc, and Special lobs a Cinder Cluster.
