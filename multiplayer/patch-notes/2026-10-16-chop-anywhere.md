area: Woodcutting
title: Chop wherever you like
---
- Trees no longer shrug off your axe when you start a new cut somewhere else. Every spot you chop keeps its own notch, and you can go back to any of them.
- Felling still takes three solid cuts in the same spot, and the tree falls from the notch that took the third.
