- Skeletons now come in six warband looks: **Mossward**, **Rustguard**, **Thornhood**, **Old Bones**, **Bloomcaller** and **Antlerbound**, each with its own headgear, shield and colours.
- New model with a hinged jaw, glowing eye sockets and textured bone, cloth, wood and iron.
- Shields hang relaxed at their side and only come up to block when you get close, or after they attack or are hit.
- They walk with planted feet, rake at you with a clawing swipe, chatter their jaws, limp on one leg and crawl with none.
- Shield size now depends on the look, so a small buckler covers less than a tower shield.
