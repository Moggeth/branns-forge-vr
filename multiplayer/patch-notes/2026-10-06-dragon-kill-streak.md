- New kill streak: land the **killing blow on 5 skeletons in a row** without falling and you earn a **dragon**. Five flame pips at the top of the screen fill as you go, and falling resets them.
- Press **H** (or tap **Call the Dragon** on a phone) whenever you like. A big, friendly red dragon swoops down out of the clouds, you hop into the saddle, and it circles high over the spot you called it from for **20 seconds**.
- Aim by looking around: a glowing ring on the ground shows where you are aiming, and the view turns with the dragon so the battlefield stays in front of you.
- Three attacks:
  - **Fire Breath** (hold click, or hold the button): a stream of fire that scorches everything within 3 m of the ring and sets the ground alight. About 2.5 seconds of breath per tank, and it refills when you let go.
  - **Fireballs** (right-click): three fireballs that land in a tight triangle, 60 damage each, every 1.4 seconds.
  - **Comet Dive** (Space): the dragon dives at the ring and blasts everything within 7.5 m for 190 damage, then climbs back up. Every 6 seconds.
- Up in the saddle you cannot be hurt, and your feet stay where you left them. When the ride ends the dragon sets you down with 2 seconds of protection.
- Kills you make from the dragon count for experience, but not towards your next dragon.
> The name is a placeholder for now. On phones, the walking stick and weapon buttons step aside during the ride and the three dragon buttons take their place. In VR the dragon waits for you on a screen, for now.
