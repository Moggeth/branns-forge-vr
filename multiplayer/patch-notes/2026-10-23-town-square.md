title: The town square
---
- **Your keep's heart is now a proper town square.** Round-laid cobbles fan out from the heart forge, and strings of warm lights run from its crown to ribboned poles. They glow brighter as night falls. Heroes now step out facing the square instead of the gate.
- **The training ring.** Two straw dummies stand in a sanded ring. Whack them with anything and they rock back. The coach waiting by the talent board opens your weapon talents when you press **E**.
- **The bakery cart.** A baker works beside a glowing bread oven. Press **E** at the counter for a free apple, straight into your hand (three bites of health).
- **The bard's stage.** Press **E** at the stage for a tune. There are four little jigs, and everyone in the square hears the same one. The lights twinkle, the dummies sway and the townsfolk dance along.
- **The welcome board.** The board by the gate road shows the town news (these patch notes).
- The square is busier too. Strollers do their rounds, a small kid runs the other way, and the baker, coach and bard keep their stalls.
- Grumblekeep has its own goblin version of everything, from the grumpy goblin baker to the goblin bard.
