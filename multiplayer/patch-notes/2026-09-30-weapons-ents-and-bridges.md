- Added **Emberwing**, a physical bow that looses phoenix arrows shaped by your draw.
- Added the **Stonewhisper** pick and the **Swallow Line** grappling tool.
- Cutting a tree now leaves a stump and drops real lumber you can pick up.
- Added a skull basket quest: carry skulls home and deliver them.
- You can jump, and walk straight through portals.
- In VR, held items are steadier without dulling your throws.
- Added **Awaken Tree**: trees wake up as Ents, fight, and drop fruit you can eat and seeds you can plant.
- Heroes can build walkable timber bridges with rails and piers.
- God spells now run on Mana.
- Snow settles on the world instead of flickering.
- Nine zones were rebuilt with new art, including Gloamfen, Sky Garden, Starfall Ridge, Ember Foundry, Mushroom Glade and Pinewood Ruins.
