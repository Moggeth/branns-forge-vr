title: The Stormreel and the Stormspire
---
- New building: the **Stormspire**, a stone lightning tower with a crystal capacitor in its hat. Build it from the workshop (8 Stone, 6 Copper, 1 Gold, 2 Crystal).
  - Feed it Crystal to charge it. Each Crystal stores four full shots, up to twelve.
  - A God's **Lightning** bolt landing on or beside it fills it to the brim instead of hurting it.
- New weapon: the **Stormreel**, a lightning crossbow with rubber grips that plugs into a cable-reel backpack. Walk up to a Stormspire and press Use to shoulder the pack.
  - Hold Attack to charge (the glass tubes fill and sparks climb the horns), release to fire. A short tap fires a weaker shot that costs less charge.
  - Each bolt trails a fine copper thread, and the tower's current runs down it, through your target and into the ground. Foes standing on the ground take a huge shock that fells most skeletons in one hit, and anyone within 2.5 m of them is jolted through the soil.
  - Foes off the ground have no path to earth, so they only feel a little static. Rain makes every shock half again as strong.
  - An empty tower still fires a plain bolt.
- The pack holds **30 metres of cable**. It drapes over the ground behind you, tightens as you reach the end, and stops you walking further from the tower.
- Choose any other weapon and the pack reels itself home to its hook. Press Use at the tower to hang it up yourself. Taking or hanging the pack drops in one of your Crystals when the tower has room for it, and on keyboard **R** beside the tower feeds it another.
> Rubber grips, a grounded circuit, and a proper fuse box. Brann insists it is perfectly safe.
