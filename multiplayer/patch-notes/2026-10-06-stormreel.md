title: The Stormreel and the Stormspire
---
- New building: the **Stormspire**, a stone lightning tower with a crystal capacitor in its hat. Every home base now has one standing beside the gate, fully charged, and you can build more from the workshop (8 Stone, 6 Copper, 1 Gold, 2 Crystal).
  - A crew of **lizards in little hard hats** looks after each tower. One climbs the copper coil checking the windings, one minds the crystal hopper and one naps on the hat brim. They hop for joy whenever the tower is charged.
  - Feed it Crystal to charge it. Each Crystal stores four full shots, up to twelve.
  - A God's **Lightning** bolt landing on or beside it fills it to the brim instead of hurting it.
- New weapon: the **Stormreel**, a lightning crossbow with rubber grips that plugs into a cable-reel backpack. Each tower carries two packs, a **red (+) positive** one and a **blue (−) negative** one, and each comes with its own crossbow: the red one is crimson lacquer with bone-white bands, the blue one is blackened steel with electric-blue bands. Walk up to the pack you want and press Use to shoulder it.
  - Hold Attack to charge (the glass tubes fill, sparks climb the horns and the nose starts to crackle, with a capacitor whine that rises as it charges), release to fire. A short tap fires a weaker shot that costs less charge.
  - Each bolt trails a hair-thin glowing filament from your crossbow, with little pulses of current running along it. Every hit is a full shock that fells most skeletons at once, flying or not, and anyone within 2.5 m of the strike is jolted through the soil. Rain makes every shock half again as strong. Walls stop the bolt.
  - **Close the circuit:** every live bolt stays lodged where it lands as a glowing red (+) or blue (−) probe with a little badge floating above it. A red crossbow only ever fires positive bolts and a blue one only negative ones, and current only flows once **one of each** is lodged, in either order, no more than 28 m apart. Then lightning roars between them, red fading to blue, and everything on the line is fried for as long as the tower has charge. Foes on the line crackle and glow.
  - On your own: fire a positive bolt, drop the pack (switch weapon, or hang it up), walk to the other hook, shoulder the blue pack and fire a negative bolt. The first bolt **stays connected** the whole time, held to the tower by its cable, for 90 seconds. Drag it more than 65 m from the tower and the cable lets go. With a friend you can each wear a pack and fire at once.
  - Press **Q** (or Special) to **reel your bolt back in** along its filament. It rips free of whatever it is stuck in, zips home to your crossbow, breaks any live circuit and returns 60% of its charge to the tower.
  - An empty tower still fires a plain bolt.
  - New sounds: a rising charge whine, a crack and sub-bass thump on release (bright and rising for the positive bolt, low and falling for the negative one), the bolt biting in, a thunderclap and a held electric buzz while a circuit runs, a ratcheting winch as a bolt is reeled in, the snap of a cable letting go, and a low transformer hum beside charged towers.
- The pack holds **45 metres of cable**. It drapes over the ground behind you and **wraps around trees** as you walk past them, using up cable. Walk back around the tree and you get it back. At the end of the cable it tightens and stops you walking further.
- Choose any other weapon and the pack reels itself home to its hook. Press Use at the tower to hang it up yourself. Taking or hanging the pack drops in one of your Crystals when the tower has room for it, and on keyboard **R** beside the tower feeds it another.
> Red to blue, rubber grips on, and never lick the coil. Brann insists it is perfectly safe.
