title: Keep buildings show the damage you deal
---
- Hitting a keep's pylons, spires, ballistas or heart now kicks out stone chips, sparks and a flash where you struck.
- A small health bar floats over whatever you just hit and drains smoothly, so you can see how close it is to falling.
- Pylons and spires crack open as they weaken. Glowing cracks appear at two-thirds health and spread at one-third, and a pylon's crystal starts to stutter.
- A broken pylon or spire bursts into rubble.
- Keep stonework now shrugs off plain crossbow bolts and arrows, which only chip it for a quarter of their damage. **Golden bolts** still hit for full magic damage and burst with a gold rune ring, and melee, spells and siege engines are unchanged.
> Sieges were far too easy from range. Bring a hammer, a God, or some luck with the golden songbird.
