title: Blown sky-high
---
- Explosions now blow skeletons clean apart. Every bone flies up and away from the bang, and the skull sails highest of all. Powder kegs, bursting Kettlebellies, firebombs, dragon fireballs, dragon dives and golden-bolt bursts all do it.
- Golems caught in a blast are thrown apart into tumbling rubble instead of dropping in a heap.
- A bang also scatters any bones and rubble already lying nearby.
- Powder kegs and Kettlebellies now go off with a proper billowing fireball, a rolling column of smoke, a shock ring along the ground and a scorch mark that slowly fades. Kettlebellies burst in a cloud of steam instead.
