date: 2026-11-11 16:30
area: Weapons
title: Your first forged weapon comes sooner
---
- **The Siegebird now costs 6 Wood and 4 Iron** (was 8 Wood, 4 Iron). One iron seam and a tree or two is enough, so most heroes forge it within their first five minutes.
- **The forge smelts as it forges.** Iron ore you're carrying now counts toward a weapon's Iron, so you no longer have to wait for it to be smelted first.
- **The Emberwing now costs 10 Wood, 4 Iron and 2 Crystal** (was 12 Wood, 3 Crystal), and **Sunfall costs 8 Iron and 4 Crystal** (was 10 Iron, 5 Crystal). Crystal growths are rare near the village, but the first shrine you reach pays out enough for the Emberwing.
- **The Wilds guide now shows you where to look.** If you're short of Iron it points to the nearest iron seam, and if you're short of Crystal it points to the nearest crystal growth, shrine or ruin, with the distance and direction.
