title: Dawnhold's gates are guarded by Timberlines
---
- Dawnhold's two gate ballistas have been replaced with **Timberlines**, standing on brass-rimmed stone daises that glow blue while a pair sits on the rails.
- Man one with Use and it handles exactly like a Timberline you build: swing it with the mouse, sight along the brass ladder, click to fire and hold R to wind it faster.
- The keep pays for every bolt, so you never spend Wood, Iron or crafted bolts at the gate.
- The twin bolts cut through every skeleton in their path, fell trees and stick fast like any other Timberline pair, and they also slice rival Heroes for 45 damage. The Ballista Captain's Hat still halves the wind and adds half again to every pair.
- Left alone, a gate Timberline still tracks and fires on its own, winding up 50% slower than a crewed one. Grumblekeep keeps its Gnashjaws.
