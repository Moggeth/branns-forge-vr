title: The Watch-Arch gets a proper tower and an owl
---
- The Watch-Arch has been rebuilt in weathered sandstone. Its tower now has a battered base, a crenellated top knocked down on the east side, arrow slits, and a studded oak door up two steps on the west.
- The beacon basket is now a bronze owl with big crystal eyes. When you feed the watch-fire, its eyes light up.
- The watch-fire now glows the way a fire should after dark. It has a soft halo, sparks drifting up, and a pool of blue firelight on the grass around the tower. By day the glow stays gentle.
- Feeding the fire, the alarm and rising at the hearth all work exactly as before.
