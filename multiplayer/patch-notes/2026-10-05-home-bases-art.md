title: Dawnhold and Grumblekeep get their proper look
---
- Every building in both home bases has been rebuilt in full detail.
- **Dawnhold** is pale dressed stone, polished brass and glowing blue crystal. Its heart forge has a real hearth, an anvil, bellows and a brass cradle holding the floating heart. Its timber-framed barracks has a slate roof, banners and a brass bell.
- **Grumblekeep** is dark basalt, old bones and bubbling green ooze. Its heart is a skull-fronted cauldron forge under a ring of curling ribs. Its barracks is a rib-roofed hall with a goofy buck-toothed skull over the door and a gong by the entrance.
- Both bases now have a proper gate arch where the road leaves the dome, and a ring of boundary stones round the edge. The gate pillars and boundary stones are solid.
- The fountains shimmer, the heart rings spin, ballistas load a fresh bolt after every shot, and the barracks bell swings (or the gong shakes) whenever a squad marches out.
> Both bases draw in only a handful of batches, so phones should not slow down.
