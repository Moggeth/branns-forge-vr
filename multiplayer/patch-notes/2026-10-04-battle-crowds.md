- Worlds now have room for **32 players**. Bots use a separate budget.
- An optional **Battle mode** pits Dawn against Dusk: protect your sanctuary, advance with your soldiers, and destroy the opposing sanctuary. The host can turn it off to return to the normal adventure.
- Each army sends four soldiers every 15 seconds, up to 16 living soldiers per side. Heroes fight alongside them; Gods can support allies and strike enemy forces.
- Remote Hero animation does less repeated work while preserving the same poses. Large-room performance still depends on the host device and everyone's connection.
