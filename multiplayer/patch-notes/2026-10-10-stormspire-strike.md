title: Stormreel: one strike, one kill
---
- **Closing the circuit now kills whatever it hits, instantly.** Land a red (+) bolt and a blue (−) bolt in the same foe and the tower's power arcs through it and burns it down on the spot, golems included. No more waiting for the current to cook it.
- **The strike is a very big deal.** The Stormspire dumps its charge down the cable and a roaring violet arc welds the two bolts together through your target, spraying molten sparks. It is so bright that the world around it goes dark for a moment, and the bang and the roar carry right across the map. It looks and sounds like a power line shorting out, not a thunderstorm, because the power is coming down your cable.
- **The cable now routes around everything it touches**, not just trees: keep buildings, gate ballistas, walls, rocks, the Forge and the tower itself. Walk back the way you came and it unwinds.
- **Each keep's Stormspire now stands at the front of the fort**, just inside the gate, so the cable leads straight out to the fight.
- **The Stormspire can no longer be destroyed**, and foes stop wasting their time swinging at it. A tower knocked down in an older save stands again.
- On phones, Special now reads **Reel** and Drop reads **Spike** while you hold the Stormreel, and Collect picks a spiked crossbow back up.
- The dragon streak counter no longer covers your Stormreel or Timberline ammo plate.
> If your system asks for reduced motion, the flash and the darkening are toned down.
