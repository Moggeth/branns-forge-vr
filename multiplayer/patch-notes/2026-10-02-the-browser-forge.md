headline: The Browser Forge
---
- Brann's Forge now runs in your web browser, with nothing to install and no account needed.
- **Create a world** and invite up to seven friends with an 8-digit code or an invite link.
- Each player picks **Hero** (first person, or VR on a headset) or **God** (an overhead strategy view), and can switch any time from the Menu.
- The world runs at a steady 60 ticks a second for everyone.
- Your world saves itself in your browser, and you can download a backup.
- If the host leaves, another player can choose to carry the world on.
- Added a welcoming, themed lobby.
