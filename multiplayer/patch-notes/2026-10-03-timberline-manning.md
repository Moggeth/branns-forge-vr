- Look at a Timberline and press **E** to take the seat. The prompt shows what a shot costs.
- Aim with the mouse: a full 360° swing on a heavy carriage, tilting from −8° to +25°. Click to fire along the barrel. **E** or **Esc** steps off.
- It winds itself back in 4 seconds. Hold **R** to wind twice as fast, and a second Hero holding **R** beside it makes it four times as fast.
- The forge can now craft Timberline bolts: 2 Wood and 1 Iron makes 4 shots. Crafted bolts are used first.
