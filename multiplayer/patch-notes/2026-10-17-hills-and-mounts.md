- **The Great Wilds got its hills back.** Between the plains you'll now find rolling hill country, quiet dales tucked between the rises, and patches of terraced ledges stepping up the slopes.
- The land is dressed in stone: **tors** crown the hilltops, **rocky outcrops** lean out of the ledges, **boulder fields** spill across the downs, and weathered **rock stacks** stand among the high crags. They're solid, so they make good cover and good landmarks.
- **Mounts!** Two saddled horses wait just outside the village forge. Walk up and press **E** (**Collect** on phones) to climb on, and press it again to hop off. A horse runs about twice as fast as you do and jumps a little higher.
- Somewhere in the forest near the village, a **wild tiger** is lounging. It won't let you ride it just yet. Pick up an apple and offer it from your hand, and you'll have a friend for life: quick on its feet, and it leaps higher than anything else on four legs.
- Your mount waits right where you leave it and grazes nearby. Other players see you up in the saddle.
> Mounts live in The Great Wilds and The Underways for now. Expedition runs are still travelled on foot, and Brann's Valley is a bit small for a gallop.
