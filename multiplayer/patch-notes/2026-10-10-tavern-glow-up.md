title: Taverns and Quartermaster wares glow-up
---
- All three taverns are cosier inside: a wagon-wheel candle chandelier, plate rails and tankards on the walls, a notice board of bounties by the door, herbs and garlic drying over the bar, a braided rug, and a trophy over every hearth (The Muddled Mug finally hung up its legendary trout).
- Tavern windows and lanterns are now leaded glass that glows like candlelight instead of molten rock.
- Hearth fires lick and flicker with embers drifting up, and every chimney puffs lazy smoke you can spot from across the keep.
- Outside, firewood is stacked by each chimney, roses and ivy climb the Dawnhold and Muddled Mug porches, and Grumblekeep sprouts goofy glowing toadstools.
- Every Quartermaster item has a hand-made picture in the shop instead of an emoji, and the Healing Draught and Hearty Ale got their own art too.
