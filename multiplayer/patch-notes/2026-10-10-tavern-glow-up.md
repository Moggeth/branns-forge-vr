title: Taverns and Quartermaster wares glow-up
---
- All three taverns are cosier inside: a wagon-wheel candle chandelier, plate rails and tankards on the walls, a notice board of bounties by the door, herbs and garlic drying over the bar, a braided rug, and a trophy over every hearth (The Muddled Mug finally hung up its legendary trout).
- The walls got proper panelling and exposed beams inside: Dawnhold paints its wainscot cottage blue, Grumblekeep a cheerful toad green, and The Muddled Mug keeps honest honey oak. The plaster is a clean, warm limewash now.
- New furniture everywhere: plank tables on turned legs, high-backed settles by the walls and ladder-back chairs with cushions, with supper laid out (bread and cheese, berry pie, and in Grumblekeep a bowl of something glowing).
- Behind every bar: a panelled back with a carved crest, tankards hanging on hooks, and corked, labelled bottles. The tankards themselves got a proper lip, foot and hoops.
- All the livery is new: heraldic banners with trim, fringes, tasselled cords and a raised device (Dawnhold's sun over an anvil, Grumblekeep's skull and crossbones, The Muddled Mug's foaming tankard), plus livelier bunting and a tasselled Quartermaster awning.
- Tavern windows and lanterns are now leaded glass that glows like candlelight instead of molten rock.
- Hearth fires lick and flicker with embers drifting up, and every chimney puffs lazy smoke you can spot from across the keep.
- Outside, firewood is stacked by each chimney, roses and ivy climb the Dawnhold and Muddled Mug porches, and Grumblekeep sprouts goofy glowing toadstools.
- Every Quartermaster item has a hand-made picture in the shop instead of an emoji, and the Healing Draught and Hearty Ale got their own art too.
