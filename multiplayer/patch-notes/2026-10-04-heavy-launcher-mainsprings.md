title: The Heavy Launcher, rebuilt around twin mainsprings
---
- The **Heavy Launcher** has a brand new body. Two big blue-steel spiral mainsprings in brass drums now drive the throwing arm.
- Every part does its job. After each shot a cocking lever lifts and presses the arm down, the springs visibly wind tighter, the arm clicks under its trigger and a fresh stone drops into the cup.
- On loosing, the arm whips up, slams into the lever's bar and the whole frame shudders on its buffer. The stone now leaves from the cup itself instead of the middle of the machine.
- The crystals on the axle and lever glow brighter as the springs take up tension.
- Its power, cost and reload time are unchanged. It's still comically strong.
> The new Heavy Launcher is also a real, working catapult you can 3D print, springs included.
