title: A brighter welcome
---
- **The title screen has had a glow-up.** You now arrive in a sunlit valley: Brann's forge puffing smoke on its hill, a river winding past, birds overhead and a crystal humming in the grass. The choices sit in one forge panel beside the logo.
- **Play tells you where it's taking you.** It names the world you'll continue, or tells you a fresh Great Wilds is waiting. New world… and Play with friends sit right under it.
- **Opening a world has a proper loading screen,** with Brann's hammer ringing on the anvil and a handy tip while you wait.
- **New world… got painted cards** for the Great Wilds, Brann's Valley, the Expedition Roguelite and the Underways. The map preview hangs in a frame, and Open to friends is now a clear switch that says who can join.
- **Brann's Valley and the Expedition Roguelite now explain themselves** in New world… with three quick steps, instead of a blank box.
- **On phones, New world… fills the screen** and the Start button always stays in view, upright or sideways.
- **The world menu matches.** Back to the world is the big friendly button, the guides sit side by side, and each group of settings has its own panel. Nearby voice no longer shows in a world that's just you.
