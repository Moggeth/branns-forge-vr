- The axe's edge now carries a glowing blue resonance line, and its metals look worn and real.
- The axe shows its own cooldown: light refills from the blossom, down the vine and along the edge, and a quiet whetstone "shing" plays when it's ready.
- The axe special is now a **Cleave**: one level sweep, 4.5 m deep across 140°.
  - Cuts down every skeleton in the arc in one hit.
  - Throws golems back.
  - Fells up to four trees in front of you, which drop their usual logs.
- The Cleave no longer digs into the ground or leaves stones behind.
- Special cooldown increased to 6 seconds (was 4) to match its new power.
