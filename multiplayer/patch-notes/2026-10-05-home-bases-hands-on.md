title: Take the reins at home
---
- **Man a gate ballista.** Walk up to either ballista at your own base and press E. Swing it with the mouse (or drag on a phone), click to fire, and hold R (or Wind) to winch the next bolt in faster. Bolts are free and fly straight and true. While someone is at the wheel, the ballista stops firing on its own.
- **Ring the barracks bell.** Press E at the bell beside the barracks door and a squad marches out right away. The bell needs a little over a minute to settle before it rings again.
- **Drink from the fountain.** A long drink heals 40 health on the spot. The fountain takes 20 seconds to refill.
- **Mend with your hammer.** Strike your own damaged pylons, spires and ballistas with the hammer to patch them up. Mend a broken pylon and the dome comes straight back.
> Stand next to any of them and a hint tells you what it does. Heroes from the other side can't touch your toys.
