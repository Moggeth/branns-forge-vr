title: The Pick of Riches, reforged
---
- **A whole new Pick of Riches.** Same faceted pick you know, rebuilt from the steel up as an assayer's tool turned weapon: a blued deepsteel head with glass-hard white points, a gold seam hammered down the spine of each arm, sapphires set right through the steel, two heart stones in the eye, a crown of crystal on top, a hickory haft with gold straps and an oxblood grip, and the assayer's stamped tag swinging from the pommel.
- **The crystals are real crystals now.** They bend the world behind them, throw little sparks of fire as you turn the pick, and catch the sun with a glint on the points and crown.
- **Every hit rings.** Strike stone, ore or crystal and the ring races up the gold seam into the heart, the points flush forge-hot for a moment, and shards and sparks spit off the rock. Rich veins shower gold dust.
- **It knows where the riches are.** Near an ore deposit the gems quicken and the heart beats, faster the closer you get and stronger for the rich stuff.
- **It glows with you.** The pick gives off a soft blue light that grows at night, and flares when the special is ready, with a shiver and a chime.
- Stand still for a while and your Hero lifts the pick to admire it.
- On phones the gems use a lighter version of the new look and the pick skips its own light, so it costs about what the old one did.
> Gold carries the resonance. That's why the seam lights up.
