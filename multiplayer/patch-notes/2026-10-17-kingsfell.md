title: Kingsfell, the new axe
---
- The axe has been reforged as **Kingsfell**: a broad war axe of ring-patterned steel that grows out from a glowing amber heartstone, with gold sap veins running from the heart to the edge. As your special recharges, the heart wakes first, light flows out along the veins and the edge ignites. When it is ready, the heart beats.
- New special, **Timberfall**. Hold Special to raise Kingsfell overhead and see the lane it will cut, with a ring at the foot of every tree it will bring down. Release to hew the ground. A standing blade of amber light tears 19 m down the lane. Every tree in its path falls away from you in turn, foes are cut down as the light reaches them, and golems are hurled down the lane. Everything right around you is still cleaved as before.
- **Heartwood:** every tree Timberfall fells is split to its heart, and some hearts hold treasure: amber, and now and then gold or crystal, left by the stump. Every cast that reaches a tree brings up at least one amberheart, and the best of it waits near the far end of the lane.
- **Amber** is a new material. Collect it like any other.
- **Sapdrinker:** every axe blow that bites a tree or a foe brings Timberfall back half a second sooner.
- Timberfall recharges in 7 seconds (was 6). With the Whirlwind talent it cuts in a full circle around you with 30% more reach and recharges in 5 seconds (was 4).
- Kingsfell got a full finishing pass, and the forge left its story all over it. Its steel carries the growth rings of the king-oak it was forged from, a single oak leaf is still sealed deep in the amber heartstone above a glowing ember, and specks of sap twinkle inside. A gold crown and a vine-wrapped socket mark it as royal work, the smith's crowned-tree stamp sits on the poll, tally marks for every giant it has felled are inlaid on the haft, and an amber acorn hangs from the pommel on a braided cord.
- The axe is about 20% smaller to download, even with all the new detail.
> Pick of Riches turned rock into treasure. Kingsfell does the same for the forest.
