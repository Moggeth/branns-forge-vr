patch: 2026-10-03b
---
- Rooms are now hosted in the cloud, so friends can join from anywhere.
- Players connect to each other directly when they can, and through a relay when they can't.
- If a host drops suddenly, the game notices quickly and offers the world to another player.
- **Play solo** works even when room hosting is down.
