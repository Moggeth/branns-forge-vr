- Stand at the forge and it smelts all the ore you carry or have stored, in batches side by side, instead of one ore per click.
- Bars now come out on a stone output bay, one labelled stack per metal. One press picks up the whole stack, for example "Pick up 7 Iron Bars".
- The forge has a new furnace with a glowing hopper ring, a chute and "ORE IN / BARS OUT" signs.
- The Stores tab opens on Smelting, with **Smelt all ore**, furnace progress and **Collect all**.
- The hotbar now uses square slots, with a round God/Hero switch (**F9**) on its left.
- The Build tab fits on one screen as a grid of tiles, with All and Ready filters.
