title: Wyrmspout reforged
---
- **Wyrmspout** has been reforged as a sleek steel wyrm with a glass tank of liquid fire on its back. Its jaw drops open as it sprays, and the tank and a gauge facing you show how much fuel is left.
- It now sprays a thick jet of **sticky fire** that arcs about 10 to 13 m and breaks into globs. Each glob deals 5 damage and sticks to whatever it hits.
- Globs that land on a foe keep burning it for 2 seconds, and each extra glob makes that fire burn a little hotter, up to 6 globs. Globs leave burning puddles on the ground and burning patches on walls and trees.
- A full tank lasts about 1.75 seconds of spraying. An empty tank refills in 3 seconds. Let go early and it starts topping up after a short pause.
- New special, **Gout**: lob a ball of fire that bursts into a wide burning splash, sticking fire to every foe within about 2.4 m. It uses a third of the tank and has a 6 second cooldown. It replaces the old heat vent.
- New sounds for the jet catching, sputtering out, running dry and refilling. Phones and VR controllers rumble while you spray.
> The tank on top doubles as your ammo counter: watch the liquid drain while you spray.
