title: Larimar Cove and Shellbright the turtle
---
- Each keep's back town now has a hidden cove tucked into the cliffs: a little turquoise lake with lily pads, reeds, cattails and a flat basking rock.
- **Shellbright** lives there, a sea turtle with a shell of sky-blue larimar set in a notched silver crown, and flippers of hammered silver scales.
- Shellbright basks on the rock, slides into the water with a splash, swims laps with a slow, flying stroke, and comes up to breathe with a little puff.
- Stand at the water's edge and Shellbright may swim over to say hello, watching you, blinking, and sometimes yawning.
- At night the larimar shell glows from within and lights the water around it, and fireflies drift over the lake.
- You can wade into the shallows, but the deep middle belongs to the turtle. Shellbright is peaceful and can't be hurt.
> Inspired by a much-loved larimar pendant.
