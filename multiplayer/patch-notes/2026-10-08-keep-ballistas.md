date: 2026-10-08
area: Weapons
title: The keep ballistas get a glow-up
---
- Each keep now guards its gate with its own siege engine, and they shoot real bolts that fly, drop and stick instead of hitting instantly.
- **Sunlance (Dawnhold):** a gleaming brass and blue-enamel ballista with feathered wings and a sun-shield sight.
  - Hold to draw. The wings bend back, the sun ring lights up and the lance flies faster and hits harder (50 to 120 damage).
  - Let go on the golden beat after a full draw for a **Dawnbreak**: a golden lance that pierces up to four skeletons and bursts in a blaze of sunlight at the end.
  - Lances stay stuck in whatever they hit for a while, skeletons included.
- **Gnashjaw (Grumblekeep):** a horned skull on a spiked slate plinth that spits a barbed harpoon on a chain.
  - A harpooned foe is held on the chain. Turn the crank to reel them in, click to yank them closer, and once they're near enough the skull **chomps** them for 120 damage.
  - Big golems are much harder to reel in.
- Both have a thin aiming arc and landing ring while you're sighting, plus a range readout in metres.
- A friend can help wind the reload (or reel the chain) for a big speed boost, just like the Timberline.
- When nobody is manning them, the ballistas now aim properly: they track, lead walking targets and fire real shots.
- A hero caught by an unmanned Gnashjaw can tear free by running past the end of the chain. A manned one holds on.
- New synthesised sounds for drawing, loosing, harpoon hits, chain rattles, the reel ratchet and the big chomp.
