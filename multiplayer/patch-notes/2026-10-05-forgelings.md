title: The forgelings get real bodies
---
- **Dawnhold's troops are forgelings now.** No more skeletons in borrowed blue paint. Each one is a stout little stone-and-brass fellow with a bobbly head, big glowing crystal eyes, a brass grille for a jaw and a crystal heart that shines through the chest.
- **Every squad is a bit different.** Forgelings turn up with a smoking chimney hat, brass goggles or a tool belt, and they all carry a round forge shield.
- **They still fight like they always did.** Same swings, same stumbles, and yes, you can still knock bits off them.
