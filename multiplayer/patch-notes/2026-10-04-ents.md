- New God spell **Awaken** (18 mana, **O**): wakes the nearest tree within 8 m.
- New God spell **Wild Awakening** (40 mana, **P**): wakes up to 3 trees in a 7 m circle.
- Young trees become quick little sapling Ents, and mature trees become slow, heavy elders. The tree shudders, pulls its roots out of the ground and opens glowing blue eyes.
- Heroes can fight them: branches come off one at a time and drop logs, axes deal double damage, fire spreads onto them, and felling one drops real wood.
- An Ent nobody defeats roots itself as a tree again after about a minute.
