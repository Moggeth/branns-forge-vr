- Old stumps now wake up as **Stumplings**: little root critters wearing their stump's own apple, oak or birch bark, with glowing blue eyes and a sprout on top.
- Most wake 3 to 10 minutes after a tree is felled. They waddle around their old spot and run from Heroes who come close. They never attack.
- Chop one for a log, plus Copper Ore about 25% of the time and Gold Ore about 5%. Axes deal double damage to them.
- Left alone, a Stumpling burrows away after 90 seconds.
- Either way, the original tree grows back from a sapling.
- Fixed chopping failing for good once a world had felled 256 trees.
