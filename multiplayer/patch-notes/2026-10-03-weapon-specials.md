- Press **Q**, right-click or tap **Special** to use your weapon's special.
  - The axe winds back and bowls a sundering strike forward.
  - The Stonewhisper pick raises a curved crystal wall that you can mine back down.
- Press **E** to collect nearby lumber. In VR, grab a log and drop it in your waist pouch for two wood.
- Boulders and meteors lose real chunks as you mine them.
- Mountains now mark the edge of the world, and the ground you walk on is exactly the ground you see.
