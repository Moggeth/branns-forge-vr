- The **Ancient Stone Sanctuary** wakes up. Strike a standing stone with any tool to ring its note and light its rune for 25 seconds. Ring all eight together and the bronze hoop opens a **waygate** for 60 seconds, restoring Heroes inside the ring (+30 health, +40 mana).
  - Walk through the gate to travel to the Forge, the Crystal Outcrop, the Watch-Arch, or the fallen star during the Expedition. A glowing circle at the far end brings you home.
  - Strike the altar to choose the next destination.
- The **Crystal Outcrop** is a living geode. Crack its six crystal clusters with a pick for Crystal; broken clusters regrow over time.
- The **Watch-Arch** beacon can be lit. Strike its offering dish with Crystal in your pack: one Crystal burns for 5 minutes, banking up to 15.
  - While it burns, it sounds a horn and marks every foe with a light, and fallen Heroes rise at its hearth.
- God-side Influence obelisks are now **Heartstones**: living geode hearts that mend nearby Heroes of their side, more strongly as the world's Bloom grows. Wildflowers spread around them as Bloom rises.
- Every landmark has been rebuilt with hand-modelled, textured stone, bronze, timber and crystal.
- The testing supplies pile no longer appears in new worlds.
