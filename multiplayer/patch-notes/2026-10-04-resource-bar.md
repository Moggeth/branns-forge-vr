title: Your supplies, always in view
---
- A new strip under the hotbar always shows every material you carry, each with its own little icon: Wood, Stone, Crystal, all five ores and all five metals.
- Materials you have none of are dimmed. When your side's stores hold some, a small "+N" shows how many are stored.
- Counts give a little hop whenever they go up.
- It stays visible as both Hero and God.
- On phones it sits along the bottom edge and shows Wood, Stone and Crystal, plus anything else you're carrying.
