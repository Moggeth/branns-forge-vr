- Starfall meteors now land, leave a crater and stay in the world to be mined.
- You can play the Hero and God views side by side, and move or resize the second view.

- Gods have six summons and quick-cast gestures.
- Skeletons plant their feet, and their attacks land where their weapons actually swing.
- Mining now happens in stages and drops loot you can carry.
- Every kind of tree can be chopped.
- Arrows stay in the bone they hit, even after a limb comes off.
