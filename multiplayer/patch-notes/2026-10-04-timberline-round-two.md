- Bolts now fly on under gravity for up to 12 seconds (they used to vanish about 40 m out), so long artillery shots work.
- A bolt that hits something it can't cut sticks there, and its twin hangs from the wire. A tethered golem is dragged and slowed. The newest 60 stuck pairs stay in the world.
- While seated, two glowing blue arcs show where each bolt will land, with the distance and flight time.
- Hold right-click to look through a new brass **scope** with range marks, a rangefinder, shots left and a winding ring.
- An ammo plate shows what you have, for example "12 bolts + 7 from Wood/Iron", and the machine no longer shows bolts loading when you're out.
- New bolts, new textures on every part, and blue tracers in flight.
- **Phones can man the Timberline:** Attack fires, hold Wind to crank, Scope zooms and Collect steps off.
