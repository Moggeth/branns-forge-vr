title: Weapons are stashed and drawn
---
- Switching weapons in first person now plays a quick stash and draw, about half a second in total, and every weapon has its own style:
  - The **axe** is hefted onto your shoulder, then comes back down in a short chop.
  - The **pick** swings down to your hip, then comes up and gets a prospector's tap.
  - The **hammer** sags away under its own weight, then is heaved up and lands with a thud.
  - **Siegebird** is rolled onto its side and slung, then drawn canted to check the rail.
  - The **Emberwing** bow is turned flat onto your back and keeps its own sweep up from below.
  - The **Wyrmspout** tips its nozzle up before it goes, and arrives with a sputtering shiver.
  - The **flask** is tucked into your belt, and drawn with a toss and a catch.
- You can't swing, fire or use a special mid-swap. Change your mind halfway and the weapon you were putting away comes straight back up.
- Put Siegebird away while a songbird is still bringing your bolt and the bird now veers off, drops the bolt and flies away instead of vanishing. Take Siegebird out again and a bird brings a fresh bolt; you can fire once it is seated.
- In VR, weapons still change in your hand instantly.
