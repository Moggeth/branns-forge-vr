title: Weapons are stashed and drawn
---
- Switching weapons in first person now has a quick stash and draw. Your current weapon drops out of view and the next one rises into your hands, taking about half a second in total (0.25 s down, 0.3 s up).
- You can't swing, fire or use a special while a swap is in progress. Change your mind halfway and the weapon you were putting away comes straight back up.
- The Emberwing bow keeps its own sweep up from below. In VR, weapons still change in your hand instantly.
