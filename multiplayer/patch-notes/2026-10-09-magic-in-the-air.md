title: A little more magic in the air
---
- **Glittering snow.** Settled snow now sparkles with tiny ice-blue and golden glints that flash as you move, and puddles after rain catch the light the same way.
- **Twinkling snowflakes.** A few falling flakes catch the light and flash as they drift past.
- **Fae lights by day.** A sparse drift of soft gold and green lights floats over the meadow in daylight, and the blue resonance sparks still come out at night. They hide during rain and storms.
- **Crisper snowy days.** Snowfall is no longer a flat grey-white haze: the air is cooler and clearer, the colours hold, and you can see further.
