title: Forgelings at work: timber and ore hauled home
---
- **Put your Warband to work.** While commanding your Forgelings, right-click (or tap) a tree, an iron seam or a crystal growth and they set to it. They swing their hammers at it, chip by chip, until it gives.
- **Timber.** They fell the tree together, wait for the trunk to come down, then shoulder the logs one each.
- **Iron and Crystal.** They break the seam or growth and hug the lump to their chest.
- **They haul it home.** Each load goes back to the Forge, or straight to your Hero if you are closer, and lands in your stores. Then they go back for more and work every tree or seam nearby until the area is bare, then stand guard there.
- **Watch the road.** Anything that jumps them on the way gets a hammer in return, and then they carry on.
> Round two of the RTS revival: your Forgelings now feed the forge. Next up: new kinds of troops, starting with the Sapper.
