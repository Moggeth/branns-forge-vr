title: Nearby voice, easier to use
---
- Added a one-tap voice button. On desktop it sits in the top bar; on phones it's the round microphone at the top right. Tap once to listen in, again to talk, and again to mute.
- A glowing speech badge now floats over anyone you can hear while they're talking, and your own button pulses green while you talk.
- Taverns are now voice rooms. Everyone inside hears each other clearly across the room, voices from outside sound muffled through the walls, and walking in invites you to join the chat.
- Voice can now relay through the room service when two players can't reach each other directly, which is common on mobile data. This switches on once the relay is set up on the server.
- The voice list in the menu now shows who is talking.
> Voice is still opt-in: joining only listens, and your microphone stays off until you turn it on.
