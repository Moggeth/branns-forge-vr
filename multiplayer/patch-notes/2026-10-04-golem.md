- Golems are now made of the world's own stone and bound together by blue resonance light. A crystal heart glows through their chest and crystal spires break out of their backs.
- Their feet stay planted as they walk. They shift their weight, swing their arms, and every step thuds and kicks up dust.
- The slam hums and flares as it winds up, then lands with a dust ring, a shockwave, a boom and a rumble if you're close.
- Hits make a golem stagger. One that loses a leg lurches on, and one with no legs drags itself by its arms.
- Defeated golems now drop 1 Iron.
- The golem model is about 7× smaller to download, which helps phones.
