- The Orchard floor is now a meadow: grass sways in rolling gusts and parts around Heroes, with drifts of wildflowers and golden pollen in the air.
- Sunlight glows through tree leaves, so canopies no longer look like dark blobs.
- The world's edge is now forested alpine foothills with snowy peaks behind them.
- Added twelve glinting **ore veins** around the foot of the mountains:
  - Copper and Iron are common, Gold and Mithril are uncommon, and Star veins are rare.
  - When a vein is mined out, a new one appears somewhere else 3 to 5 minutes later.
- **Copper** and **Gold** are new metals you can refine at the forge.
