title: The Fusewick: a Forgeling with a powder keg
---
- **New troop: the Fusewick.** Cast **Muster a Fusewick** (key H, 40 mana) and one Forgeling drops in with a powder keg strapped to its back and its goggles down. You can have two at a time.
- **Point it at trouble.** Select it and right-click (or tap) a foe. It scampers up, strikes its fuse and stands there fizzing for a moment, keg rattling, then the whole thing goes up.
- **A big bang.** Everything hostile nearby is blown apart: skeletons go flying and break, and rival walls and keeps take a real dent. The ground is left with a scorched crater.
- **Your own are safe.** The blast never hurts your Forgelings or your side.
- **Handle with care.** A Fusewick has less health than its friends, never fights and never hauls. If something knocks it down on the way in, the keg goes off where it falls.
> Round three of the RTS revival: your Warband now has a sapper for the moments a hammer won't do.
