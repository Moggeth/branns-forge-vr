title: Oaks and daisies from a real walk
---
- Oak trees now wear real bark, made from a photo of an old oak trunk.
- Ivy climbs the oak trunks and spreads around their roots. It was cut straight from a photo of ivy on a fence.
- Little clumps of daisies, cut from a photo of daisies in the grass, now grow along path edges and in patches of lawn. They sway in the wind and bend when you walk through them.
- Ivy is just for looks: your axe still bites the bark underneath.
> All three come from photos Mitch took on a walk.
