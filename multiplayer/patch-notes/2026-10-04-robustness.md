---
date: 2026-10-04
area: Bug fixes
title: Safer saves and steadier battles
---
- Killing a Stumpling or Ent with arrows no longer prevents the world from being loaded later.
- Disconnecting while carrying ore no longer duplicates that ore when you rejoin.
- A dropped direct connection can now recover through the room's relay without treating your friend as having left.
- Timberline's aiming guide follows rising and sinking terrain, and leaving the game window releases its held zoom.
- Broken skeleton shields release unused memory when their creature disappears.
- Multiple Phoenix bursts fade independently instead of making older birds flash bright again.
- If a resumed multiplayer world's ground is too full to drop old players' supplies, an inventory recovery copy is saved before the new world autosaves. Those supplies remain in the recovery copy, not in your new pack. If the copy cannot be saved, resuming stops with instructions for keeping your original save safe.
