title: The Warband: troops of your own to command
---
- **New Summon spell: Muster Forgelings (= key, 30 mana).** Three shafts of forge-light stab down from the sky and three Dawnhold Forgelings drop down them, landing in a spray of brass sparks. They are your own troops: they fight for you and never for the other side.
- **Command them from above.** Press **G**, click one of your Forgelings, or tap the new **Warband** button to take command. A brass ring lights up under each one you command. Right-click (or tap) the land to march them there, or an enemy to send them after it. Shift-click a Forgeling to pick it out on its own. Esc or G lets go.
- **They hold what you give them.** Forgelings sent somewhere stand guard when they arrive and fight anything that comes close, then walk back to their post. Wild skeletons, golems and awakened Ents will go for them too, so a quiet spot can turn into a real battle.
- **They escort your Hero.** Come down to the land and every Forgeling standing guard falls in behind you, jogging to catch up if you run ahead. They pile into any fight near you. Tap **Rally** to call them back to your side.
- **Their wins are your wins.** Kills by your Forgelings give your Hero experience, and they hit harder as your Hero levels up.
- From above you can see each Forgeling's health and where each one has been sent.
- You can command up to six, so phones and headsets keep their frame rate.
> First round of the RTS revival. Next up: Forgelings that chop timber and haul ore for you.
