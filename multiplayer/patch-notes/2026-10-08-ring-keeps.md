title: Ring keeps and their back towns
---
- Dawnhold and Grumblekeep have been rebuilt as ring keeps. Each now sits in a wide, level bowl cut out of its corner mountains, with the heart forge in the middle and the barracks, tavern, mill and Quartermaster standing in a half ring around it.
- The gate, ballistas and fountain face the battlefield, so the front of each keep stays open for the fight while the town stays tucked safely behind the heart.
- Behind each keep, a narrow lantern-lit pass runs between the cliffs into a walled back town, tucked into the corner of the map past where the old edge used to stop you.
- The back town's Workshop is your keep's own smithy: walk up to the anvil with ore in your pack and it's smelted into bars one by one, sparks and all. No more trekking out to the Forge with every load.
- The Stores next door bank everything you're carrying, just like the Forge or a Storehouse.
- Two cottages and a well round out the yard. Only your own keep's Workshop and Stores will work for you, so it's worth defending the pass.
- Fixed the keep stone (plaza, gate, heart plinth and wards) showing up near-black instead of its proper stone.
