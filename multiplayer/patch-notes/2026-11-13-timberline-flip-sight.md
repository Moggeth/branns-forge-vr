title: A clear view from the Timberline
---
- **New flip-up sight on the Timberline.** The tall brass ladder and its number plates are gone. In their place is a slim hinged leaf that lies folded flat while the machine stands idle, then swings up on its push-rod the moment someone takes the seat.
- **Range reads straight off the sight.** Two slim brass rails frame an open window, crossed by hairline bars. Each bar is a range from 25 to 300 metres. Put your target on the glowing pip in the middle of a bar and the pair comes down on it. The numbers sit on small tabs outside the rails, well away from what you're aiming at.
- **The bars show how wide the wire is.** Two tiny glowing ticks on each bar mark the span of the tether between your bolts at that range, so anything between them gets caught. A foe about a wire wide that fills the ticks is at that range.
- **Nothing sits on your target any more.** While you're manning a Timberline, the weapon bar, hint line, supplies and the floating "Step off" prompt all step out of the way, and the distance readout that floated over the landing ring is gone. The plate at the top now shows your ammo, the reload and the Step off key, plus a quick reminder of the controls when you first sit down.
