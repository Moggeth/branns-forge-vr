title: The keeps grow into towns
---
- Dawnhold and Grumblekeep have stretched their walls. Every building is bigger, and there's room for a little town inside.
- **Gold!** Felling enemy troops in a keep battle drops Gold into your purse. Golems pay three times as much as skeletons.
- **The Quartermaster** has opened a stall in each keep. Press E at the counter to browse a shelf of eleven items, from Springheel Boots and a Pocket Kettle to the Ballista Captain's Hat and the Heartstone Locket. You have six pockets, your items stay with you through every fall, and anything sells back for half.
- **The Gold mill** creaks into life. Tip gold ore, copper ore and stone into its hopper and it grinds them into Gold, coin by coin, out of the chute. Hold R on the capstan to grind three times as fast. Leaning on it is very satisfying.
- **The healing well actually heals now**, and you can see it. It also fills a Healing Draught you can carry into battle. Press V (or tap the flask) to drink it.
- **Three taverns** are open: The Gilded Anvil in Dawnhold, The Rattling Tankard in Grumblekeep, and The Muddled Mug just south of the Forge, shared by both sides. Nobody can be hurt inside a tavern, nobody inside can hurt anyone, and monsters get shown the door. Sit a while, mend, have a natter, and ask the bar for a Hearty Ale: full health, then blows land 20% softer for three minutes.
> Unmanned keep ballistas now reach 17 m (was 22 m), so from their new, wider spots they still guard the same ground around the heart. Crewed ballistas are unchanged.
