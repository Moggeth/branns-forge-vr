pr: 31

- The world now has a full day: about 13 minutes of sunshine, then dusk and a dark 7-minute moonlit night. One day lasts 20 minutes.
- At night the sky fills with stars, a moon and a faint blue aurora, blue sparks drift near the ground, and the world's own crystals, gems and forge glow light the way.
- Every 4 minutes the weather can change: clear skies, fog, rain, thunderstorms with lightning and thunder, or snow.
- New God spell **Rainfall** (**R**, 30 mana): 3 minutes of rain over the whole world, stacking up to 6 minutes.
- Any rain makes groves grow Bloom twice as fast, and Heroes slowly heal while it falls.
- Everyone in a world sees the same hour, the same weather and the same lightning strike.
