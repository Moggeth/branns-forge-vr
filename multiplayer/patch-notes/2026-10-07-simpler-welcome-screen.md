title: A much simpler welcome screen
---
- The welcome screen is now just your name, Hero or God, a big **Play** button, How to play and Patch notes.
- Play drops you straight into the busiest open world near you, or starts a fresh one if they're all full.
- Got an invite link or scanned a friend's QR code? Just press Play and you'll land in their world. If it has closed, you'll join another world near you instead.
- The code box, world list, solo button, saved worlds and equipment guide have been taken off the welcome screen to keep things simple.
