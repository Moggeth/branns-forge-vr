title: The Pick of Riches
---
- The Stonewhisper pick's ridge now tears real minerals up out of the ground. Each stone along the ridge is granite, iron, copper, crystal, gold or mithril, and you can see which at a glance.
- Every ridge brings up at least one rich seam of crystal, gold or mithril. Most of the rest is good honest stone, iron and copper.
- Mine any part of the ridge to collect what it's made of. Gold seams glitter, mithril shimmers, and a rich seam rings out with a little treasure chime as it breaks the surface.
- A ridge left standing until it crumbles now drops shards of its own minerals instead of plain stone.
> Star metal stays a prize for meteors and the deep mountain veins.
