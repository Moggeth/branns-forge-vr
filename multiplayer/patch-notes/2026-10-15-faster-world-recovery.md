- When a direct connection stalls, the cloud connection now steps in after a quarter of a second instead of eight seconds.
- Joining while the host is leaving now asks another player to carry the world immediately.
- Fixed a misleading backup warning when the world had not arrived yet. Actual backup failures still show a warning.
