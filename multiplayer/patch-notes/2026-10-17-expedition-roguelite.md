title: Expedition Roguelite (experimental)
---
- New in the **New world** menu: **Expedition Roguelite** (experimental), a short, replayable adventure across a freshly generated world. The usual valley is unchanged.
- Choose a starting kit at camp (Woodcutter, Prospector or Skirmisher), then pick your route across a branching map of destinations: skirmishes, elite golem packs, falling star shards, ward circles to hold, quiet shrines and Underways entrances. Beat the guards at an Underways mouth and the party heads down into a real dungeon for its hoard.
- After every destination each Hero picks one of three upgrades for the rest of the run, and Gods pick God upgrades. Then the party votes: **return to camp** and bank the whole haul, or **push on** for a 25% bigger haul with more danger.
- The final destination is the Heart. Its guardian changes from run to run, so check the route map before you commit:
  - **Meteor Colossus**: a crowned golem nearly twice the usual size. It hits like a landslide.
  - **Bone Warden**: antlered in bone with a glowing green soul gem. While it stands, the dead keep climbing out of the ground around it.
  - **Shardback**: crystal spines down its back. Three glowing heartstones mend it until you crack them.
  Beat the guardian to bank your haul twice over.
- Look out for the **Wandering Smith** on the road. No fight: spend Embers from your haul on an upgrade, or keep them and walk on.
- Every run rolls an **omen** that bends the rules: **Blood Moon** (more foes, 30% more haul), **Gilded Road** (foes drop extra Embers, smiths sell at 40% off), **The Long Road** (one fewer spare life, but every destination heals you fully and offers a fourth card) and **Starfall** (Star Shards pay double). Some runs get Clear Skies instead. The Daily Expedition always has an omen.
- Five new upgrades: **Scavenger**, **Bulwark**, **Windrunner**, **Second Wind**, and **Starwell** for Gods.
- The party shares its spare lives. Run out and a quarter of the haul still makes it home.
- Banked Embers stay with you between runs. Spend them in the new **Hall of Embers** on permanent unlocks: a spare life, a head start, rerolls, a fourth upgrade card, extra supplies, and new upgrades for your card pool.
- The **Daily Expedition** gives everyone the same seed each day, and the game keeps your best haul for the day. You can also type in any seed to replay a run exactly.
- Every new expedition rolls a new world, and replaying a seed brings back the same world and route.
- In VR, the wrist panel shows the run and lets you pick kits, routes, upgrades and return or push on with the trigger.
