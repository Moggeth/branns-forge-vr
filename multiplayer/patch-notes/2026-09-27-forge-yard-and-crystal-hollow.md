- Brann's Forge Yard was rebuilt as a proper sheltered workshop, with clear stations, an upgrade path and porch supply bays you can walk straight up to.
- Added the Crystal Hollow, a crystal vault with a tool challenge that remembers your progress.
- Added the first explorable Orchard loop: seeded groves, varied ground and a valley full of trees.
- Added a hand-made forge lantern with a hinged shutter.
> These first patches were the original desktop VR prototype. Much of it was rebuilt for the browser version you play today, so a few of these features look different now or are still waiting to come back.
