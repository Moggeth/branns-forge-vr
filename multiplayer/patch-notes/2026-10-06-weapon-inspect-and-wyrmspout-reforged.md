title: Weapon Inspect, and the Wyrmspout reforged
---
- New: **Weapon Inspect** for every weapon. Tap **I** and your hero lifts the weapon into view and turns it over: one side, the top, a long stare down the business end, then the other side. Tap again to put it away.
  - Hold **I** and move the mouse to turn it yourself.
  - On phones, it's **◈ Inspect** in the Actions menu. Drag to turn the weapon, and tap Inspect again to put it away.
  - Attacking, using a special or switching weapons puts it away straight away.
- The Wyrmspout has been reforged with a new head: a jade-scaled wyrm with heavy scowling brows, swept ivory horns, crimson frills and a mouth full of teeth.
- The liquid fire in its tank now really sloshes. It stays level as you turn, piles up against the glass when you run, jump or stop, and rocks back and forth before it settles. Spraying stirs it up and drains it as you watch. Inspect the Wyrmspout and spin it to play with it.
- The Wyrmspout now tells you how it's doing with its own body:
  - **The Wyrm's Eye** on the back of the tank looks at you. A wide, round pupil means a full tank and a thin slit means you're nearly dry. It squints while it breathes fire, blinks now and then, and shuts while the tank refills, opening again as it fills.
  - **Three fins** on its neck light up one by one as Gout recharges, and glow softly when it's ready.
  - **Little flames** flicker in its nostrils whenever Gout is ready.
- New scaled, hammered-brass and glass materials for the Wyrmspout.
> The old brass ring around the neck is gone; the fins do that job now.
