- The blobby "bonsai" trees are gone. The Orchard now grows three real species with leafy crowns:
  - **Apple:** a rounded crown with red apples.
  - **Oak:** broad and dark, with acorns.
  - **Birch:** white bark, a light airy crown and catkins.
- Trunks flare into the soil on rounded roots, and crowns sit high enough to walk beneath.
- Distant trees were rebuilt too, so the forest still looks like trees from across the map.
- Chopping, felling, logs and Ents all work exactly as before.
