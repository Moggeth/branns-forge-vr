title: Roots, embers and golden rewards
---
- Stumplings now grow their tree exactly where they burrow, including after saving and rejoining.
- Phoenix arrows ignite the surface they strike. Nearby ground can catch fire from falling embers afterward.
- Walk up to an impacted golden Siegebird bolt and Collect it for **1 Gold Bar**. Each bolt can be collected once.
- Tree chopping, mining fractures and fire effects are prepared while loading to reduce first-use hitches.
