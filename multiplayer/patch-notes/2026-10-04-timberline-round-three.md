title: Timberline, round three
---
- **Bolts no longer stop in mid-air.** Ore, rocks and buildings used to have invisible hit zones far bigger than the models (ore was 2.5 m tall around a 1.15 m rock). Every hit zone now hugs its model, and stumps and the old standing stones catch bolts too.
- The blackout scope is gone. Hold right-click (or Q) to sight along a new **brass range ladder** on the machine, with a light zoom and the world still all around you.
- Each rung of the ladder is marked 25 to 300 m. Put a target at that range on the rung's silver pip and the pair hits it.
- The aim arcs are now very thin pale lines that only appear while sighting, ending in a small brass ring with the distance.
- No more blue mark in the middle of the screen while seated.
- Q now works while seated at the machine.
