title: Blueprint placement
---
- Choosing a building now shows a glowing blue **blueprint** of it wherever you aim, so you can see exactly how it will stand before you spend anything. It turns red and tells you why when it can't go there.
- Left-click places it. Right-click or Esc backs out. A tooltip at the bottom of the screen shows the controls.
- Press R (or scroll the mouse wheel) to turn the building. Hold Shift as you click to keep building the same thing, which makes laying rows of foundations quick.
- You keep your crosshair while building, so there's no more freeing the mouse to point at the ground.
- The blueprint stays within building reach, so it never drifts out of range when you look into the distance.
- **Phones:** drag to look around and tap the ground to move the blueprint, then use the new Place, Rotate and Cancel buttons. Tapping no longer builds by accident.
- **VR:** the blueprint follows your controller. Pull the trigger to place, or squeeze the grip to cancel.
