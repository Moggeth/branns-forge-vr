title: Livelier skeletons and smoother crowds
---
- Enemies now glide smoothly between network updates instead of surging and coasting, and they turn in smooth arcs rather than snapping to face you.
- Skeleton feet stay planted where they land. No more skating when they turn, back off or sidestep, and side-steps and backing away now take real steps.
- Crowds flow around each other: a skeleton that finds a friend in its way steps round them instead of shoving, so warbands fan out and surround you rather than piling up in a queue.
- Walkers face where they're going and slow down to turn, so bodies and feet always agree.
- Every warband walks like who it is: Rustguard march with their knees up, Thornhood creep low and quick, Old Bones shuffle and stoop, Bloomcallers skip along with their arms out, Antlerbound strut with their chests puffed, and Mossward amble with a rolling sway. Thornhood are a touch quicker on their feet and Old Bones a touch slower.
- A skeleton that spots a Hero now gives a startled little hop, jaw agape, before squaring up.
- Dawnhold's forgelings march in a steady drill step of their own.
