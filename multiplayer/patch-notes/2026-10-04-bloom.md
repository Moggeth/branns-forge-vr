- Terrain changes are now **unlimited**. Terrain spells no longer stop working after 64 changes.
- Almost every God spell now leaves the Heroes a gift:
  - Raise Earth and Stone Wall leave a boulder to mine, Lower Earth exposes an iron seam, and Sunder leaves crystal shards.
  - Grove grows two healing apples, and Healing Rain puts an apple on up to 3 nearby groves.
  - Lightning near a Hero hurts (25 damage) but **charges** their weapon: the next 3 melee hits within 15 seconds deal +40.
  - Frost Nova makes nearby Heroes **swift**: 40% faster for 6 seconds.
  - Tree Ring becomes a sanctuary that heals 3 health per second.
- New world **Bloom** meter. It grows as life thrives: groves, apples eaten and foes felled by Heroes.
- Bloom unlocks three new spells:
  - **Life Spring** (Bloom 30, **Y**, 16 mana): a glowing pool that heals 4 health per second for 60 seconds.
  - **Blessing** (Bloom 90, **U**, 20 mana): Heroes within 6 m heal 20 and gain an 8-hit charge.
  - **Quake** (Bloom 180, **I**, 24 mana): foes within 8 m take 30 damage and are slowed, and ore shakes loose. Heroes nearby take 8 damage, so aim with care.
- Every spell now has a sound, and every gift has a visual and rumble cue.
> Feeding the Heroes earns the God nothing directly. Bloom measures how much life the world holds.
