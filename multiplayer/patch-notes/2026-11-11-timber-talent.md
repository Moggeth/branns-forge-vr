---
- **New axe talent: Timber!** A tree you fell now crushes every foe it lands on and throws them clear. Lure a few skeletons under a big oak, take your third cut and watch them go flying.
- Rank I hits for 60. Rank II gives a wider crash that hits for 90. Rank III gives the widest crash, hitting for 130. Your level and your axe's temper make it hit harder still.
- The hit lands the moment the trunk touches the ground, with a burst of bark and sawdust along its length.
- The axe now has three talents to choose from: Limbsplitter, Whirlwind and Timber!
