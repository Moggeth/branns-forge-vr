title: The Brinedeep salt mines
---
- Two salt mines have opened in the mountains, one in each of the open corners between the keeps, the same distance from both sides. Follow the track up the cutting, through the timbered portal and down the tunnel into a glowing pink rock-salt hall.
- **Rock salt** is a new resource. Seams glow from inside like a salt lamp and pay 3 Salt every few swings. The rose-red **salt heart** pays 10. Tip Salt into a Gold mill for 3 Gold apiece, more than gold ore.
- Mined seams grow back in a minute and a half to two and a half minutes, so the mines are worth coming back to and worth fighting over.
- **Mind the roof.** Every swing strains the rock. Grit trickles down and a dark ring spreads on the floor where the roof is about to go, giving you under two seconds to step clear. A cave-in deals 30 damage and leaves a pile of salt rubble you can dig out.
- **Firedamp** seeps from cracks in the hall floor as a faint yellow-green haze. Bring a flame near a full vent (Wyrmspout, a lit Emberdew, a fire arrow) and it goes up in a fireball: 46 damage to anyone within about 4 metres, more cave-ins, and a shower of loose salt with a fresh salt heart cracked open. It takes a couple of minutes to build up again.
- **Saltback crabs** live in the brine pools. They scuttle sideways, pinch hard if you crowd them and flee when hurt. Crack one for Salt and a little Hero XP.
- **The mine goes deeper.** Two drifts lead on from the hall. One climbs to the **Carvers' Chapel**, a tall gallery carved entirely out of salt: a statue of Brann himself, a waving Saltback, a goat enjoying the world's biggest salt lick, a carved wall telling the mine's story and a glowing salt chandelier. The other drops to the **Brine Deep**, a lake chamber hung with salt icicles, where the richest seams grow and salt hearts turn up twice as often. Mind the third firedamp vent down there.
- The walls are real rock salt now: cubic blocks in every shade of pink and milk-white, each catching your lantern on its own, with the salt's warm light bleeding out through the cracks.
- Keep your ears open for the mine canary. While it sings, the air is good. If it keels over, the firedamp is full, so put that torch away (or don't).
- Some Saltbacks are more equal than others. Now and then a crab climbs out of the Brine Deep's lake wearing a tiny gold crown. **King Pinchy** is bigger, grumpier and far tougher, and he pays 10 Salt.
- Rumour has it that somewhere past the far end of the lake there's a gap only a brave Hero would squeeze through, and something golden at the end of it that is very, very good at producing salt. Give it a knock.
- A few other odds and ends are lying about for the curious.
- Inside, daylight, weather and wind give way to the salt's own warm glow and your lantern.
> The mine's light stays phone-friendly: the salt glows by itself, and your lantern is the only light in there.
