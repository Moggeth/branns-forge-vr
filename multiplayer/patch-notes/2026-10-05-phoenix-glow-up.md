title: The phoenix catches fire
---
- Emberwing's phoenix is now a **real bird**: head, body, beating wings and long tail streamers, instead of the bare pair of wings it used to fly on.
- A curved phoenix shot now burns a **bright arc across the sky**. The flight plume is about twice as long as a plain arrow's and far hotter, so you can read the curve from across the valley.
- The **wing trails** are wider and painted like flame rather than pale light: a white-hot centre with soft edges that waves, lifts and cools to deep orange as it falls behind, and sheds drifting embers while it burns.
- Phoenix arrows now **bloom into fire where they bite**. The hit flashes, a ring of fire opens across whatever was struck, and burning feathers are thrown back out of the wound and fall around it, before the usual burn takes over.
- All of it keeps its colour in daylight. The old trails washed out to pale yellow over a sunlit field.
> The fire ring lies flat on whatever you hit, so you can tell a body shot from a tree hit at a glance.
