title: The big sound pass
---
- The valley sounds alive now. Songbirds trade little tunes by day, crickets chirp and a curious owl hoots at night, and a soft breeze rises when the God looks down from on high.
- Keeps have their own soundscape: walk up to a tavern to hear chatter, clinking mugs and a lute; fountains trickle and the mill creaks.
- Weapons whoosh as you swing them, and your feet make soft footsteps on grass, cobbles, snow and puddles. Jumps, landings and tool swaps have sounds too.
- Skeletons clatter apart when they fall, popped-off bones go "pok", and getting knocked out or coming back now sounds like it.
- Battles finally have a voice: horns when they start, drums for each wave, a fanfare for victory, plus sounds for shields going up and down, turrets firing and keep pieces breaking.
- Added sounds for the forge, workers, smelting, Expedition stages, goo, timber saws, meteor eggs hatching, phoenix fire catching, and Wake the Woods and Wild Wake.
- Every button in the game now gives a soft click.
- New mixing: everything shares one balanced mix that never crackles on phone speakers, the scenery quiets for a moment under huge hits like quakes and falling stars, and big multiplayer fights limit how many sounds stack up.
