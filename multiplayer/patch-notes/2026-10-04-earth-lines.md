- **Raise Wall** (was Stone Wall) now grows a 3.2 m geode rampart behind a racing blue seam.
  - Its ends ramp up so Heroes can climb it, the rising front throws enemies clear and lifts Heroes onto the top, and every section can be quarried to open a breach.
- **Sunder** now tears open a 2.4 m chasm lined with crystal.
  - Its front wounds and staggers enemies, and it leaves crystal seams on the floor to mine.
- Both now last one minute, instead of leaving permanent lumps and trenches. The wall shatters into stone and crystal shards, and the chasm closes but its seams stay.
- A God can keep three of each standing. A fourth replaces the oldest.
- The ground rises and opens at the same pace you see it, and both spells have new sounds, rumble and an exact aiming preview.
