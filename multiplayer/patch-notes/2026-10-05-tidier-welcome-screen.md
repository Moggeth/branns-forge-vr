title: Press Play to join a world near you
---
- **No more room codes.** The big button on the welcome screen is now simply **Play**. It drops you into the busiest open world in your part of the world, so the first 32 adventurers to press Play end up together. If every nearby world is full, or nobody near you is playing yet, it lights a fresh world for you instead.
- Worlds are grouped by region (Americas North and South, Europe and Africa, Asia, Oceania), so you're matched with players on a decent connection rather than someone on the far side of the planet.
- Want a particular world? **Public worlds** still lists every open world by its host's name, and invite links from friends still work.
- **Tidier welcome screen.** The buttons along the bottom no longer squash together on phones: Saved worlds has its own line, with How to play, Patch notes and Equipment underneath as three even buttons.
- The Hero and God descriptions on a sideways phone now show in full instead of trailing off with "…".
