title: A tidier welcome screen
---
- The buttons along the bottom of the welcome screen no longer squash together on phones. Saved worlds now has its own line, and How to play, Patch notes and Equipment sit underneath as three neat, even buttons.
- The Hero and God descriptions on a sideways phone now show in full instead of trailing off with "…".
- "Browse public worlds" is now simply **Public worlds**, so it fits on one line next to Create a world.
- On computers, the join box gets the full width so the code field is never cut off, and the guide buttons stay on one row.
