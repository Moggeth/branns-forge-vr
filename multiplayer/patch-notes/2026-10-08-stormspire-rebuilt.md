title: Stormreel, Stormspire and Voltnewts, rebuilt
---
- **The Stormreel crossbow has been rebuilt.** A sturdy faceted receiver, a recurve prod in steel yokes, twin glass capacitor tubes on top where you can watch them fill, a copper coil round the bolt and lightning horns that arc as it charges. The red crossbow is crimson enamel with bone trim; the blue one is blackened steel with electric-blue trim.
- **It feels like a machine now.** The limbs snap forward when you fire and ring down, the string flies to the brace line before the magnetic latch draws it back, a fresh bolt zaps into the seat, and recoil kicks the stock into your shoulder.
- **The Stormspire has been rebuilt from the stones up.** Carved stone courses, a chunky octagonal plinth, and a floppy, crooked indigo wizard hat covered in gold stars (and one patched hole). You can pick it out from across the map.
- **It shows its charge.** The glass column fills, a glow climbs the copper coil turn by turn, the hat's stars twinkle brighter, and when it's full the copper brim crackles. Close a circuit and the whole tower works harder.
- The Voltnewts now have a tiny front door and a ladder.
- **The reel packs are proper kit now:** a wooden backboard with leather straps, a brass drum with lightning-bolt spokes, a crank, a clicking ratchet and a glass lamp in your pack's colour. The drum spools as cable pays out and winds back in, a pack bounces and sways on a running Hero's back, and a pack you hang back up swings on its hook before it settles.
- **The Voltnewts got a full makeover:** painted, speckled skin with glowing veins, a new skeleton and smoother animation, wobbling hard hats and bouncing tails. Their feet now actually stand on the stone, the coil, the hopper rim and the hat brim instead of hovering near them.
- The cable now drapes in a smooth curve and bumps round tree trunks it touches instead of passing through them.
- A closed circuit now lights up the ground and the foe around it with a flickering blue glow.
> If your system asks for reduced motion, the tower's crackle and flicker and the packs' sway are toned down.
