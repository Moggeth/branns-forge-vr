title: Lanterns, laid squares and a living forge fire
pr: 111
---
- Every **lantern post** is rebuilt: a tapered post on a stone footing, iron bands, a scrolled bracket and a proper square lantern with a glass pane, instead of a plain box on a stick.
- Both keeps now have laid stone at the gate, a paved ring round the heart and a flagstone pass out the back to the back town. The Muddled Mug has two flagstone paths to its door.
- Grass and flowers no longer grow up through tavern floors, workshop floors or the keep heart's plinth.
- **Dawnhold's forge** burns with a real fire now: tongues of flame lick up over a bed of breathing coals, where it used to be a flat orange panel.
- The Forge's kiln and plinth stones and the ore you carry (iron, copper, gold, mithril, starmetal and crystal) were washing out pale. They now show their proper colours.
- The iron rock's two big dark lumps looked a bit like eyes. It's now flecked with lots of small ore instead.
- Rocks, banks and ridges are shaded smooth with crisp edges, so they stop looking faceted from up close.
