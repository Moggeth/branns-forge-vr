title: The Great Wilds and the New World menu (experimental)
---
- Press **New world…** under Play to choose where the forge is lit. **Brann's Valley** is the classic map you know and is still the default. Three new experimental worlds sit beside it: **The Great Wilds**, **Expedition Roguelite** and **The Underways**.
- **The Great Wilds** is a whole land, 4 km across, grown from a seed. Rivers start in the hills, join up and run downhill to lakes and the sea. Forests climb the slopes, marshes gather in the low wet ground, and roads wind between villages, mills, harbours and mines.
- Pick a landscape (Heartland Continent, Sunscatter Isles, Frostmere Highlands or Greenwater Basin) and a seed, and a map of your world appears before you start. The same seed and landscape always make the same world, so share it with friends.
- You start in a village at the forge with some wood, stone, apples and iron ore. A guide card in the corner names the region you're in, counts what you've found, and points you to the nearest place you haven't been yet.
- Every region has a name and its own danger. Close to the village it's safe; the further out and the higher you climb, the tougher the guardians get. Ruins, shrines, groves, camps and caches hide treasure, and some have guardians who rise when you get close.
- Trees you fell, rocks you mine and places you find are remembered in your save, even after you walk far away and come back.
- **The Underways** starts you outside a cave in the Wilds. Every cave mouth in the land leads down into its own dungeon.
- Mountains in the distance, forests on far ridges and the shine of water all stay in view, and the land fills in smoothly as you travel. Phones draw a shorter distance to stay smooth.
> The Great Wilds is still young. Villages are quiet for now (their folk are on the way), and we'd love to hear which seeds you like best.
