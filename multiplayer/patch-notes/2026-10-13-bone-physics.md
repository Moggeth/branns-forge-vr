title: Skeletons that break like real bones
---
- Skeleton bones now come apart with real physics. Loose arms flop at the elbow and wrist, legs fold at the knee and ankle, and a dead skull's jaw falls open.
- Bones break off mid-fight: a Siegebird bolt or arrow can now snap an arm or leg clean off, and a cracked limb gives way a little sooner than before. The skeleton keeps coming on whatever it has left, hopping, limping or swinging one-armed.
- Finish a skeleton with an ordinary blow and its magic lets go: the legs buckle out and it drops into a heap of bones.
- Finish one with the War Hammer (swing or slam) and it bursts apart. Bones fly from the impact the way the hammer was going, along with a spray of splinters and knuckle bones.
- Crossbow headshots land with a big hollow THUNK. The skull snaps back on its neck, chips fly, and the skeleton goes over backwards.
- Every bone clacks when it hits the dirt, and loose bones now move smoothly every frame instead of hopping between network updates.
- Fixed a bug where a skeleton that died with a foot slightly in the ground could fling a leg into the sky.
