date: 2026-10-29 19:30

title: Timber, Iron and Crystal
---
- Everything you build is now made from three resources: **Timber**, **Iron** and **Crystal**. Wood is now called Timber, and it's the one you'll use most.
- Stone, Gold, Copper, Mithril and Star metal are now extras. They show up in a small row next to your resources, but only while you're carrying some.
- No more ore to smelt. Picking up iron ore gives you Iron straight away, and the other ores work the same way. Any ore you were already carrying or had stored turns into its metal the next time you load your world.
- The supplies bar has been redrawn. Timber, Iron and Crystal each get a big labelled tile that's always visible, with a green +number showing what's in your stores. On phones the extras sit in a slim row underneath.
- Building is much cheaper, roughly half the old price, and most buildings no longer need Stone or rare metals:
  - Storehouse 4 Timber (was 6 Wood + 4 Stone). Palisade 3 Timber (was 5 Wood + 1 Stone). Bridge 2 Timber (was 3).
  - Timberline 6 Timber + 3 Iron (was 10 Wood + 6 Iron + 2 Mithril). Heavy Launcher 6 Timber + 3 Iron (was 10 Wood + 8 Stone + 5 Iron + 2 Star metal).
  - Sanctuary 3 Timber + 1 Crystal. Crystal Ward 1 Iron + 2 Crystal. Stormspire 3 Iron + 2 Crystal, so it no longer needs Copper or Gold.
  - Logging Yard, Prospector, Barracks, Watchtower, Trap and Heartstone each cost about half what they did. A Stone Foundation is 3 Stone (was 8).
  - Repairs cost 1 Timber. Six Timberline bolts cost 1 Timber + 1 Iron (it used to be 2 Wood + 1 Iron for four), and a raw Timberline shot costs 1 Timber + 1 Iron.
- Crystal growths now give 4 Crystal (was 2). Stone boulders give 3 Stone (was 2).
- New Great Wilds worlds start with 20 Timber, 8 Iron and 4 Crystal waiting at the forge yard. That's enough for a Storehouse, a Sanctuary and a Timberline before you chop a single tree.
> These prices are deliberately generous, so expect them to go up once we see how they play.
