- Mana is now the only thing that can stop a God's spell.
- Removed the hidden rules that refused casts: near Heroes or the forge, far from buildings, during an Expedition with no Hero, or past a hidden budget.
- Removed the shared 0.75 second spell cooldown.
- A cast aimed somewhere it can't go now moves to the nearest place it can, and the preview shows exactly where it will land.
- Lines longer than 18 m are shortened instead of refused, and tapping with a line spell casts a default line.
