date: 2026-11-11
area: Heroes
title: Talents, ranked up
---
- **Every talent now has three ranks.** Rank I is the talent you know. Ranks II and III push it further: Whirlwind spins wider and recharges faster, Skewer punches through up to five foes, Ember Volley fans out five arrows, Geode Burst throws its shards further, and Limbsplitter heals you for every limb you lop off.
- **Ranks open as you level.** Rank II talents open at level 6 and rank III at level 12, so there is always something to look forward to.
- **The level cap is now 20.** Levels up to 10 still give +20% health and damage each. Levels 11 to 20 give a smaller boost, but every level still gives a talent point.
- **Sunfall, the war hammer, gets its own talents.** **Earthshaker** makes your ground slam reach much further and recharge sooner. **Bonebreaker** cracks golem armour like a pick and hits golems much harder.
- **A new Talents panel.** Pick a weapon from the list down the side, then spend points on its two specialties. Each one shows its three ranks, what each rank does, and the level it opens at. Weapons with something to learn wear a gold dot. It opens on the weapon in your hand.
- **Made for phones.** Held sideways, the weapon list shrinks to icons and both specialties sit side by side. Held upright, the weapons sit in a row across the top.
- The level-up banner now tells you when a new talent rank has opened.
> Talents you already learned stay learned as rank I.
