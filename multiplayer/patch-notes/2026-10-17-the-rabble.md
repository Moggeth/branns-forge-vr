title: The Rabble arrives, and the golems found their swagger
---
- **Kettlebelly**: a runaway dwarven boiler that waddles about on piston legs, chuffing steam with every step. When its gauge swings into the red, step aside: it belches a cone of scalding steam from its spout. Crack it open and it rockets about on its own leak before bursting in a shower of rivets, copper and iron you can pick up.
- **Bramblehog**: a thorny hedge-boar with a wild rose growing out of its back. It curls into a spiky ball and bowls straight at you, skittling any skeletons in the way. Blows glance off it while it's curled, but once it stops it's dizzy and soft for a few seconds.
- **Kegtoter**: a star-nosed mole in a miner's helmet, tottering under a lit powder keg. It plants the keg by a building or a Hero and covers its ears. Hit the keg, set it alight or flatten the mole and it goes off early, and one bang sets off every keg nearby. A mole that survives its own bang cheers and digs away.
- Gods have a new Summon spell, **Rabble** (the = key, 30 mana). It calls up one of the three.
- The camp's skeletons now bring one of the Rabble along when they return.
- Stone Golems move with a lot more character. They waddle and stomp, beat their chests when they spot a Hero, knock on their own heads and shrug while idle, turn on the spot with their feet, and slam with both fists, get stuck in the ground for a moment, then heave themselves free.
