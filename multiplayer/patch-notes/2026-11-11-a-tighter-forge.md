title: A tighter, more focused game
---
- **Fewer things, done better.** We're narrowing Brann's Forge down to what's most fun: the Great Wilds, its forests and monsters, and the weapons you forge along the way. This is the first step.
- **The Great Wilds now comes first** in the New world menu, marked Recommended. Brann's Valley is still there for keep battles with friends.
- **Emberdew and Sunthread are resting for now.** They're off the hotbar while we polish the weapons that remain. If you were holding one, you'll draw your axe instead. Nothing is lost, and they may come back later.
- **Expedition Roguelite and the Underways are resting too.** They've left the New world menu while we focus on the Great Wilds.
- **Solo games have a cleaner top bar.** The invite and join-code buttons only show up when you're playing with friends.
> Want to try the resting features anyway? Add ?labs=1 to the end of the game's address.
