title: A fresh bird takes over
---
- If you fire the Siegebird and swap weapons while a songbird is bringing your next bolt, that bird flies off and a new one finishes the reload when you swap back. The new bird now always wears different colours (a robin hands over to a bluebird, a kingfisher to a cardinal), so you can tell it's a second bird.
- A golden reload's bird that gets turned away now leaves in its own feathers, so the golden bird that takes over stands out.
