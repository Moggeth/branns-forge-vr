title: Hear your friends nearby
---
- Open Menu and choose **Join voice** to listen to nearby friends, then **Enable microphone** when you want to speak.
- Voices come from your friends' direction and fade out over **32 metres**. Heroes speak from their head; God speaks and listens at the overview focus, so zooming out keeps conversations nearby.
- Voice works across Hero and God play on supported desktop, phone and VR browsers. In VR, use **B/Y** to return to Menu for voice controls.
- Mute individual friends, deafen, or leave voice from Menu. Muting, hiding the tab, and reconnecting release your microphone; turn it back on when ready.
- The game does not record voice. Some networks block direct voice connections even when the world still works; try another network if retrying voice does not help.
