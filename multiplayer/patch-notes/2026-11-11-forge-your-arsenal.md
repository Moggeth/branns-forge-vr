title: Forge your arsenal in the Great Wilds
---
- **Every new Great Wilds world now starts you with just your Axe and Pick.** The rest of your weapons are forged at the village forge, one at a time, from what you gather out in the wild.
- **The order:** Siegebird (8 Wood, 4 Iron), then Emberwing (12 Wood, 3 Crystal), then Sunfall (10 Iron, 5 Crystal), then Wyrmspout (8 Wood, 12 Iron, 8 Crystal).
- The next weapon waits on your hotbar, dimmed, with a little brass anvil mark. Press it to see what you still need. Once you have everything, press it at the village forge to forge it. It goes straight into your hands with a burst of gold.
- The Wilds guide card now tracks your next weapon: what it costs and what you're still missing.
- Forged weapons are kept with your hero's name in that world, so they're still yours when you come back.
- Brann's Valley and every world you started before this patch keep all their weapons, exactly as before.
> We wanted day one to have a clear goal: chop, mine, fight, and come home to forge something new.
