title: Apples on the ground can be picked up
---
- Apples lying in the grass now always say "Pick up", even when you're at full health. Before, they ignored you until you were hurt.
- A ground apple goes into your hand like an apple on a stick: three bites, +50 health, and the songbirds come for the core.
