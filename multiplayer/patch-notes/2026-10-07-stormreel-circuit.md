title: Stormreel, round four: close the circuit
---
- Lightning now needs a full circuit through **one** foe: stick a red (+) bolt and a blue (−) bolt in the same enemy. Bolts in two different foes, or in the ground, form an open circuit and nothing flows.
- Closing the circuit sets off a huge surge (160 damage, and foes standing close by get jolted too), then the current keeps cooking the target while the tower has charge. A cooked foe is left charred and smoking on a scorched patch of ground.
- On its own a bolt only stings now (18 damage). The big damage all comes from the circuit.
- Press G (Drop on phones) to **spike your crossbow into the ground**, butt first. Its pack sits beside it and its bolt stays live, so you can run back and grab the other pack. Press E beside it to pick it back up. Switching to another tool spikes it where you stand, too.
- A bolt that kills its target now drops to the ground with a clatter instead of hanging in the air.
- Hitting the end of your cable is no longer an invisible wall: the cable snaps taut, your crossbow is yanked back and the view jolts.
- The cable now wraps snugly round tree trunks, curving all the way round the bark instead of cutting through it or floating beside it.
- Lightning, bolts and cables got a big visual upgrade: brighter surges, sparks crawling over the target, current pulsing along a ribbed cable jacket, and new sounds for the surge, the spike and the tug.
- The charge preview flashes blue when your next shot would close the circuit.
- The Stormspire lizard crew are now **Voltnewts**: chunky teal newts with orange spikes, big amber eyes, a toothy grin, hard hats, medic harnesses and a power cable plugged into their backs. They blink, look at you when you come close, tinker with wrenches, bite the coil to zap it, nap on the hat brim, and squint, puff up and hop when the tower charges.
