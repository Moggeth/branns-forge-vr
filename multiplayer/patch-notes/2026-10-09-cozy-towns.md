title: The towns come alive
---
- Dawnhold and Grumblekeep now have townsfolk. A shopkeep waves from behind the Quartermaster's counter, barkeeps polish the bar, regulars lift their mugs, the smith hammers away at the anvil, someone winds the well, and a couple of locals stroll the square and the back yard.
- Dawnhold is kept by round-faced, rosy-cheeked folk. Grumblekeep is run by big-eared goblins in pointy hats. The Muddled Mug serves everyone.
- Barrels, crates, flour sacks, woodpiles, benches and flower boxes now sit around the taverns, shops, mills and cottages, with bunting strung across the tavern fronts.
- Worn dirt paths now link everything. A little square circles each keep's heart with paths off to the tavern, mill, shop, barracks and fountain, a lane runs up the pass to the back town, and footpaths wind down to The Muddled Mug.
- Lantern posts line the roads. Every lamp, hearth and stall lantern now gives off a soft, warm glow and casts a pool of light on the ground, and they glow brighter as dusk falls.
- The Crystal Outcrop has moved a little south, out of Grumblekeep's front yard. It no longer pokes through one of the keep's gate ballistas, and its path from the Forge and its waygate stop moved with it.
- Two mithril seams that sat in the feet of the keep ballistas now start a few steps clear of them.
- Tidied a few crates, planters and benches so they no longer poke into the back town's walls.
- Rocks are solid now. Skeletons, golems, Ents and Forge workers walk around ore rocks, boulders and meteors instead of straight through them, and so do you. Mined-out rocks stop blocking.
- The two meteors that had come to rest in Grumblekeep's lane now sit beside the Crystal Outcrop, and the sanctuary's boulder no longer leans into a standing stone.
- Ore veins and fresh seams no longer pop up right in front of a keep's gate ballistas or in the lane where the troops march. Any that already did will move on by themselves, unless someone has started mining them.
- A proper main road now runs from Dawnhold's gate to Grumblekeep's. It runs straight out from each keep, then takes two gentle 45° bends to swing round the Forge, so you can't see straight down it from one keep to the other. A lane branches off it into the Forge yard, lanterns line the whole way, and the troops march along it too.
