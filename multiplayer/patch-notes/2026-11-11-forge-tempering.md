title: Temper your weapons at the forge
---
- **Your weapons can now be tempered at the forge.** Spend Timber, Iron and Crystal to temper any weapon you own, up to three times. Each temper makes that weapon hit 15% harder, so a fully tempered Masterwork hits 45% harder. That counts for swings, bolts, arrows and the Wyrmspout's sticky fire.
- **Where:** stand by the village forge in the Great Wilds, or by the heart forge in Brann's Valley. Open your Talents (tap your level, or press N). Each weapon's page now has a forge strip at the top that shows its temper and what the next one costs.
- Talents change how a weapon plays. Tempering makes it hit harder. Both grow as you play.
- Your level badge glows when you're at the forge and can afford a temper.
- Tempering is saved with your hero, like your talents.
- The Talents list only shows weapons you've forged.
