title: Four schools of spells, all on one bar
---
- God spells are now grouped into four schools, each with its own colour:
  - **Grow**: Grove, Tree Ring, Growth Wave, Healing Rain, Life Spring, Blessing.
  - **Shape**: Raise Earth, Lower Earth, Raise Wall, Sunder, Crystal Bloom, Quake.
  - **Summon**: Skeleton, Skeleton Squad, Stone Golem, Awaken, Wild Awakening.
  - **Sky**: Lightning, Frost Nova, Starfall, Rainfall.
- On PC, every spell is visible at once. No more scrolling the spell bar.
- On phones, the spell tray shows all four schools together when you open it.
- New shortcut keys, with one keyboard row per school:
  - Grow: **1** to **6**.
  - Summon: **7**, **8**, **9**, **0** and **minus**.
  - Shape: **Q**, **E**, **R**, **T**, **Y** and **U**.
  - Sky: **Z**, **X**, **C** and **V**.
- Grove is now the spell selected when you start as the God.
> The God's purpose is to cultivate life, so Grow comes first.
