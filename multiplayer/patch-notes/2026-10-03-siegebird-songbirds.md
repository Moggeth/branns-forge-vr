- A songbird now flies in and drops each new bolt onto the Siegebird's rail. It comes from the right, then the left, and is gone before your next shot.
- Reload time increased to 1.0 seconds (was 0.7) to make room for the bird. The crank winds two turns with ratchet clicks first.
- Six natural plumages, each with a soft blue shimmer. Everyone nearby sees your birds.
- Sometimes a **golden songbird** comes instead, and its bolt deals **4× damage** with a glowing trail and a chime.
  - Starts at a 3% chance per reload and rises 0.15% with every Siegebird hit, up to 15%.
- The crossbow's walnut wood looks like warm wood again instead of nearly black.
