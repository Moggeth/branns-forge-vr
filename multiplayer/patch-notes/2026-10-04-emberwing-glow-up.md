- The Emberwing aim line now follows you smoothly every frame instead of stepping as you move, and curved arrows bend smoothly in flight.
- **Right-click is now the phoenix:** zoom with right-click to engage phoenix aim, and draw while zoomed to lock in the curve. Shots released almost straight fly straight.
- Phoenix wings now stream thin trails of light as the arrows fly and curve.
- New on **Q: Phoenix Flare.** It bursts overhead with a flash and a phoenix spreading its wings, then hangs as a star raining embers. It lights the ground and sets fire to what is beneath it. 10 second cooldown.
  - On touch and VR, Special locks the curve while drawn and fires the flare otherwise.
- A phoenix headshot that kills a skeleton now carries its skull off. The phoenix struggles with it, nearly drops it twice, then loops round and proudly drops it at your feet.
- The bow is 36% slimmer, with more detail. It rises when equipped, trembles at full draw, kicks on release and has a glowing string.
- Arrows now come in three heads and four fletchings, so arrows stuck in things look different up close.
