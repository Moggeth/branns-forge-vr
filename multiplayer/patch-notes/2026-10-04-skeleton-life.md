title: Skeletons come to life

- The skull is round and goofy again, now polished: big dark eye sockets with a small glow, a heart-shaped nose and a chunky grin.
- Pick up a skull and it turns its grin toward you.
- Defeated skeletons collapse into a jointed ragdoll that tumbles, settles on the slope and can be knocked about again. Everyone sees the same pile.
- Summoned skeletons claw their way out of the ground with a crack and a swirl of soul light, and can't fight until they're standing.
- Idle skeletons yawn, scratch, knock their skulls, stretch and, rarely, spin their heads. Each one walks with its own gait.
- Shields wear down and splinter, and the skeleton reels back when one breaks.
- The **Golden Bolt** now shatters any shield and bursts through up to six enemies with a radiant blast and twin spiral trails.
- Fixed summoned skeletons' legs sometimes vanishing when the camera looked away from the ground.
