- In first person, the tool in your hand now trails behind when you turn and springs back, bobs as you walk, banks when you strafe, and dips when you land a jump.
- Heavier tools lag more. The hammer and bones are heaviest, and the flask and bow are lightest.
- Aiming the bow or crossbow steadies it, and Reduced Motion softens it.
- VR is unchanged, because there the tool is in your real hand.
