title: Clean roads and better rocks
---
- The **main road** between the keeps is now laid stone: dressed flagstones with kerb setts along both edges, worn in places by a long life of boots. It follows the ground and re-settles when the God reshapes it.
- The footpaths out to the Sanctuary, the Crystal Outcrop and the Watch-Arch are laid flagstones too, instead of loose stepping rocks.
- Pebbles, twigs and tufts no longer scatter across the paving.
- Two earth banks that sat right on the road outside Dawnhold's gate have moved out beside it, and a root burrow and two iron rocks that crowded the road edge stepped back too.
- Iron, mithril and crystal rocks were rendering chalk white. They now show their real banded stone, rust and seams.
- The earth banks and the rock ridge in the open corner are rebuilt as weathered granite with moss and lichen, replacing the old blocky low-poly shapes.
- Fixed about 20 models that had faces pointing the wrong way, so bits of them vanished from some angles (town buildings, keep spires and pylons, the well, lanterns, trees, the dragon, crossbow wings and more).
- The ground past the keep-corner cliffs now reads as mountainside instead of a flat tan plain from the God view.
