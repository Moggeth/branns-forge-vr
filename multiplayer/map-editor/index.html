How to use it

  • Drag anything to move it. Drag empty ground to pan, scroll to zoom.
  • Keep and back-town pieces mirror: moving one moves its twin in the other keep.
  • Turn a building with its round handle, Q / E, or Shift+scroll.
  • Arrow keys nudge 10 cm (Shift: 1 m). Alt while dragging turns snapping off.
  • Red means two things overlap; orange means a steep or off-map spot.
  • Save writes the layout into the game. Then press Play-test, or Copy layout and paste it to Claude.
