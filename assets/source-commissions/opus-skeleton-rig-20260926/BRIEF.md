# Opus 5.5 skeleton evolution commission

You are the dedicated 3D artist for Brann's Forge VR. Work only in this isolated commission folder. Do not edit the live `../../godot/` or `../../service/` tree. The user loves the existing cute, stylized skeletons and their huge themed shields, but wants improved character design, proper rigging, and authored idle, movement, and attack animation. Make an actual playable asset handoff, not just concepts.

Start by reading `reference/previous-handoff.md` and `reference/actor_animation.gd`. The three existing editable Blender scenes and deterministic builder are in `baseline/`. Reference sheets are in `reference/`. Use Blender 4.5.13 at `../../tools/blender-4.5.13-windows-x64/blender.exe`; Godot 4.7.2 is at `../../tools/godot/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe`.

Make a tangible first milestone quickly: write an incremental builder and export **one** improved Mossward character with a rig and three short clips. Render and inspect it. Then refine and extend the common rig/animation solution to Prismwatch and Cinderkeep. All three should share a stable bone naming/clip contract but preserve their personalities. Mossward: curious/anxious, woodland leaves. Prismwatch: observant and lightly magical, crystalline glints. Cinderkeep: weighty smith, furnace heat. Keep ivory bones recognizable and charming, shields large and readable, silhouettes distinct. Improve anatomy and surface quality selectively, without photoreal noise or huge texture costs.

Deliver under `output/`: editable `.blend` files, self-contained character `.glb` assets, reproducible Python builder, front/side/back/hero renders plus motion contact sheets, a machine-readable manifest, and `HANDOFF.md`. Each GLB should contain `Skeleton3D` and named `idle`, `walk`, `attack` animations with sensible timing and shield/arm motion. Include shield as a distinct detachable object; ideally retain a legacy-compatible character GLB with the top-level MeshInstance3D groups `Head`, `Torso`, `LeftArm`, `RightArm`, `LeftLeg`, `RightLeg`, `Shield` if feasible, since the live runtime uses those for physics death. If that conflicts with a skinned rig, export BOTH a rigged GLB and a legacy group GLB. Never silently break the current gameplay contract. No camera/light nodes or external textures in final GLBs.

Coordinate clips with gameplay: locomotion is in-place (no authored root translation), 1 second walk cycle; attack has clear windup (0-.65), contact (.65-.8), recovery (.8-1). Shield guard remains plausible. The game has procedural bone disassembly on death, so explain how the new rig can preserve that effect. Keep the character local axes +Y up, +Z forward, +X right, toes at y=0, height about 1.5-1.7m. Shield front points +Z and must remain stealable as a standalone prop. Do not redesign existing shields beyond alignment adjustments unless necessary; they are a separate runtime workstream.

Validate imports in Godot and inspect rendered poses at idle, walk extremes and attack impact. Report polygon counts, material count, animation names, bone names, scale, whether textures are embedded, and any known VR performance uncertainty. Provide exact minimal game integration steps in HANDOFF; the coordinator will implement them in the live checkout. If you cannot finish all three, leave a tested usable first variant and honest next steps. Reflect briefly on what did and did not work in this 3D workflow so the next enemy commission can improve.
