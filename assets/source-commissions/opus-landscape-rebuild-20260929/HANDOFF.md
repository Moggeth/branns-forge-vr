## R2. Sunder/exposure hook for Sol (smallest interface)
One RGBA8 `ImageTexture` bound to `exposure_tex` on the ground material. It uses the same 320 m mapping as `macro_mask` (`uv = (world.xz - (mask_origin_x,0) + 160)/320`), and ≥1 texel/m is recommended. It defaults to black, which gives exactly the package-1 look minus the fixes.
- `r`: fresh exposure 0–1. Paint 1.0 over the op footprint plus a ~1 m feathered margin. Decay slowly in game time if desired.
- `g`: cut depth below the pre-op surface ÷ 4 m. The shader maps <0.3 m to topsoil, ~1 m to dark subsoil and >1.5–2.5 m to fresh stratified rock (`rock_fresh`).
- `b`: heaped/raised fraction (Raise Earth, Sunder rims, meteor ejecta), shown as clod soil.
- Call it from the same place each real terrain op already edits heights: `exposure.set_pixel(...)` over the dirty rect, then `ImageTexture.update()`. No terrain-op logic changes.
Caveat: in `03-sunder-after-*` the pale floor may be a separate spell mesh rather than the ground material. If so, that mesh must use this ground material (or share `exposure_tex`), otherwise nothing here reaches it.

## R3. Grow Forest tree proposal (not implemented in game)
The floating canopy in `09-grow-after-hero-eye` has fruit/blossom flecks. It is a scaled playable/grown tree, not a kit GLB, so I did not replace it.
- **Scale limit:** clamp grown-tree uniform scale so canopy top ≤ 9 m and canopy radius ≤ 3.5 m. Clamp the canopy underside to ≥ 2.2 m above ground only when the trunk is visible to it: scale trunk and canopy together, never canopy alone.
- **Placement:** reuse the existing deterministic Grow Forest positions. Skip any position within `canopy_radius + 0.6 m` of the caster (player capsule) at spawn time, and replace skipped positions with sapling `grass_tuft` or `reed_clump` scaled 1.5. This is a presentation filter only, with no new position source.
- **Presentation:** non-fruit grown trees use `tree_orchard_round`/`tall` (7 m, trunk connected to canopy) at scale 0.8–1.15. Unique apple trees and their interactions stay on their current nodes.

# Orchard Visual Kit — package 1 (terrain, river/banks, geology, vegetation integration)

Author: Claude Opus 5.5 (`claude-opus-5-5`), 2026-09-29. Visual layer only: this package doesn't change terrain, hydrology, collision, save or navigation truth.

## 1. Critique of the current captures (`input/current`)
- **Ground reads as one green blanket.** The only variation is a soft-noise tint at a single scale. No hollows or crowns, and no change from meadow to soil to rock with slope. Crests look the same as troughs. From the strategic camera, Sunder, Earthen Wall and the Crystal Fault ridge are only visible because of shading on the heightfield (see 01, 06).
- **Spell results look like decals, not earth.** The Sunder/Wall strip is a flat pale-green ribbon with cyan rectangles (01). There are no exposed walls, no fresh soil colour, no debris. The Crystal Fault is a grassy berm (06). Raised ground has to show what it's made of.
- **The river is a cyan strip.** `orchard-brook-rain.png`: the water sits level with the grass. There's no bank cut, damp margin, gravel, stones, reeds or darker depth. The shader is a sin-wave tint plus emission, which kills the sense of depth and reflection.
- **Rocks are a scatter of the same small grey lumps** (see `old-geology-contact.png`), 1–2 m across on a 320 m map. Nothing reads as a landmark. Distant mountains are stepped grey pyramids that fight the stylisation.
- **Lighting is flat and noon-white, and the atmosphere is haze-only.** No warm/cool split, so forms don't model. Pale-green "clearing" polygons (upper right of 01/06) have hard polygon edges.
- **Vegetation is placed evenly.** It's sprinkled uniformly, without groves, meadows or waterside reeds, so there's no ecological logic tied to water or slope.

## 2. Art direction and decisions (implemented)
- **Stylisation:** faceted low-poly with **flat per-face vertex colour**. There are **no textures on kit assets**, so no texture bandwidth or streaming is needed and all colour comes from the palette below. Detail comes from silhouette, strata banding and face-to-face value jitter.
- **Geology family: stratified sedimentary escarpment.** Offset slabs with bevelled edges and a 0.14 geological dip. Ledges step back from the face. Warm/cool alternating bands carry a lighter weathered tone on up-facing faces. Moss and soil sit on ledge tops, and the foot is darker and damp. Modules are designed to **overlap into a continuous scarp** at ~6 m spacing, with boulder scree at the foot. The ground shader continues the same world-Y-locked strata bands onto steep terrain (`strata_scale` = 2.35, same as the builder), so terrain rock and GLB rock read as one formation.
- **River:** the channel has to be **cut ~2 m into the bank**. Bands run from the top: grass → damp dark soil band → gravel lip with half-buried stones → bed. The water surface is opaque. Colour runs from a deep teal centre to shallow gravel-green margins, with broken foam hugging the banks, flow streaks and a Fresnel sky tint. Reeds cluster at the margins. Trees are kept ≥4 m back so the corridor stays open.
- **Ground palette:** cool hollows → lush mid → warm/dry crowns (height-weighted). Soil shows on slopes >~15°, banded rock above ~35°, and a damp band comes from `macro_mask.b`. Two-scale rotated texture sampling breaks up tiling.
- **Lighting target:** low warm key light (≈40° elevation, colour 1.0/0.88/0.72) against a cool sky fill and AgX tonemapping. Cliffs then show a lit face against a shadowed return.
- **Palette (sRGB):** grass_cool .27/.45/.22, grass_lush .40/.58/.20, grass_warm .62/.64/.26, meadow_dry .66/.60/.33, soil .42/.29/.18, soil_dark .24/.16/.10, bank_wet .20/.17/.12, gravel .55/.50/.42, rock_cap .74/.70/.62, rock_mid .58/.52/.47, rock_warm .62/.52/.42, rock_dark .33/.31/.33, moss .34/.47/.16, leaf .38/.58/.20 → .58/.70/.26, bark .34/.22/.14. The authoritative source is `PAL` in `build_visual_kit.py`, mirrored in the uniforms of `orchard_ground_visual.gdshader`.

## 5. Integration map (for Codex/Sol, into the live checkout)
Copy into `godot/assets/orchard_kit/`: `glb/*.glb`, and `godot/*.gdshader` plus `orchard_kit_materials.gd`.

**orchard_ground.gd:** replace only the `shader.code = """…"""` block with
`_material.shader = load("res://assets/orchard_kit/orchard_ground_visual.gdshader")`.
These stay intact: `MASK_PATH`, `EXTENT`, `RESOLUTION`, `material()` (texture parameter bindings unchanged), `weather()`, `sync_paths()`, `add_path()`, `sample()` and `_paint_segment()`. The uniform names are identical, and the new palette uniforms have defaults. `mask_origin_x` defaults to the existing `-360` offset.

**landscape_water.gd:** replace `WATER_SHADER` and `POOL_SHADER` code with `load()` of the two `.gdshader` files. These stay intact: `setup()`, `refresh_region()`, `_rebuild()`, `_rebuild_pools()`, `sample()`, `crossing()`, chunking, the wet-gap logic and the UV contract (UV.x across, UV.y = metres × 0.1). Optionally forward `weather()` wetness to `river_water.rain_wetness`.

**Placement (landscape_asset_library.gd / orchard_dressing.gd):** use one `MultiMeshInstance3D` per asset per chunk (25 chunks), with `material_override = OrchardKit.for_asset(name)`. Placement must sample the **hydrology and height fields only**:
- **bank_stone_lip_8m:** every ~8 m along `river_polyline`, both sides, at `±(river_width/2 + 0.4)`. Local −Y (Godot +Z) faces the water. Sink 0.1 m.
- **reed_clump:** 0.3–2.5 m outside the water edge, at 1.5–3 per metre of bank.
- **cliff_strata_*:** only where terrain slope is >35° over ≥6 m. Space ~6 m along the contour, yaw the face down-slope, and sink 0.4–0.9 m. Scatter 2–3 `boulder_medium/small` at 2.5–6 m down-slope as scree.
- **Trees:** place groves by clustered noise, not uniform scatter. Keep ≥4 m from water and off slopes >25°.
- **grass_tuft:** clustered noise mask. Skip on paths (`mask.r`) and rock.

Every asset has a buried skirt (min y −0.6 for cliffs, about −0.1 to −0.3 for boulders and bank stones), so a −0.4 m sink on slopes up to ~35° leaves no gap.

**Budgets:** see `manifest.json`. Cliffs are 2.8–6.3k tris; enable Godot auto-LOD on import and set `visibility_range_end` as listed. Everything is opaque, shadow-casting on cliffs, boulders and trees only. Turn shadows off for grass_tuft and reed_clump.

## 6. Known limitations
- The value-noise macro variation in the ground shader shows 1–2 m blockiness on flat test planes. In game it's multiplied by the real grass/soil textures; if it's still visible, swap `noise2` for a baked 256² tiling noise texture.
- Boulders are 80 tris (a deliberately faceted icosphere). The large boulder could use subdivision 3 for close VR viewing.
- Reed and tuft blades are single-sided geometry and need the foliage material's `CULL_DISABLED`.
- Godot colours render slightly darker and more saturated than Blender because ambient differs. Tune `PAL` once in the live lighting rather than per asset.
- There are no dedicated waterfall, pool-edge or river-mouth modules yet. Distant mountains (the stepped grey pyramids) haven't been replaced yet.
- The review terrain's carved channel shows the *intended* bank profile. The real bank cut must come from the hydrology carve owned by Codex/Sol (target: ~1.5–2.5 m cut, 30–45° bank, 1–2 m gravel margin).

## 7. Next serial package: spell and transformation art direction (intended, not implemented)
Shared rule: **every spell exposes what the ground is made of.** Fresh cuts show `soil` → `soil_dark` → strata rock by depth. Colour fades back to meadow over game-time via a per-chunk "disturbed" channel (`macro_mask.a`, which the ground shader already reads at 0.55).
- **Sunder:** a V-trench with jagged, faceted lips. Walls use strata shading, the floor is soil_dark, and 0.3–0.8 m broken rock chunks are thrown along both rims as MultiMesh debris. VFX: a 0.4 s dust burst of opaque low-poly particles in the soil colour, plus a ground crack decal ahead of the tear. Camera-scale screen shake only in the strategic view. Don't use cyan planes.
- **Raise Earth / Earthen Wall:** the risen mass shows `cliff_strata_low`-style ledges on steep faces, with grass kept only on the crown and a crumbling soil skirt at the base. VFX: pebbles and clods fall from the lip during the rise, and a dust ring forms at the base. The final state keeps 3–5 scree boulders.
- **Grow Forest / Tree Ring:** saplings scale in over 1.5 s with overshoot. The ground under the canopy shifts to `meadow_dry`, then leaf litter (`macro_mask.g`), then ferns. Green motes and spores stay emissive-only at low count. The clearing polygons must blend with the noise-edged mask, never hard polygons.
- **Dust, debris and fracture:** one shared opaque debris MultiMesh (3 rock-chunk variants from the boulder generator at 0.2–0.6 m) plus one soft dust particle material. Dust is the only permitted alpha: small, short-lived and unlit.
- **Starfall / meteorite:** an impact crater with a raised rim that shows rock_dark and scorched soil (desaturate and darken the albedo within the radius). The meteorite is dark iron-blue with thin emissive fissures that cool over 20 s. Ejecta rays come from the same debris MultiMesh. The only real light is one short-lived OmniLight with no shadow.
- **Crystal Fault:** a split strata ridge with a narrow glowing seam. Crystal clusters are opaque faceted geometry with emissive cores in a pale violet-cyan, a colour kept off the rest of the palette. Crystals line the seam in 3–7 clusters, not scattered dots. The ridge faces use the cliff family so the fault reads as exposed geology.
- **Atmosphere:** height fog tinted by the sky colour, stronger in the river valley at dawn and in rain. Add a distant silhouette ring of 3–4 large stylised hill/mountain cards that replace the stepped pyramids.
