# Brann's Forge hub art — handoff (2026-11-21)

## Rebuild
```
& "<blender 4.5.13>/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup -P output/build_all.py -- <input_dir_with_original_glbs> <output_dir> [hall gates kit ground preview]
```
Default input dir = parent of `output/`. The script imports `forge_shelter.glb` + `forge_detail_kit.glb` (they stay in Git) and does not copy their geometry. Textures are generated procedurally (numpy) into `output/_tex/` and embedded in each GLB. Per-run counts are appended to `build_report.txt`.

## Orientation and scale
Metres. glTF/Godot Y up, +Z toward the viewer. Builder works in Blender coordinates (x, −y, z = Godot x, z, y) and exports with `export_yup`. Every origin sits on the floor (y = 0).

## Materials (shared names across all GLBs)
Timber_Oak, Timber_Pine, Stone_Warm, Stone_Hearth, Plaster_Lime, Roof_Terracotta, Iron_Wrought, Iron_Blued, Copper_Worked (has a verdigris patina mask), Brass_Trim, Cloth_Banner, Lantern_Glow (emissive). Each material has a 256² BaseColor texture and a packed ORM texture (G = roughness, B = metallic). In main_hall the original material regions keep their names, so `.001` duplicates from the two imports are merged into one material each.

## Known limits / not done
- Hall: no props were removed. The only things in the furnace keep-out box (x −4.6..−1.6, z −6.0..−2.6) were shell or architecture nodes. Check the hearth plinth ring against the separately installed furnace and anvil; delete `Polish_HearthSurround` if it encroaches.
- The original textures were replaced with generated ones. The plaster and roof lack the hand-painted relief of the concept art, and main_hall is 4.3 MB because each GLB embeds its own copy of the textures. For production, share the textures across GLBs.
- Shading is flat with real bevels, and the UVs are box-projected (0.7 scale). There are no baked AO or lightmaps.
- Not done (no time): foliage accent, cloth/fan animation, smoke particle emitter (engineering owns this).
- Ground PNGs were generated by the first build run (`ground` step), so re-run that step if you change the seeds.
