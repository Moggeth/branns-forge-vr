# Wyrmspout v2 — handoff and tuning

The original Wyrmspout commission is still version 1 (`f040e7a4-8c6b-42a5-af56-a74cd4936a47`). Version 2 (`55b0c0ee-2622-4207-a3ca-fa006701d47c`) is a separate collection home linked to version 1. It uses Opus 5.5's reviewed GLB in `godot/assets/wyrmspout.glb` and Codex's native behavior in `godot/wyrmspout_runtime.gd`. The model is 0.404 m long with a 35 mm diameter grip at its origin; local −Z is forward. The muzzle marker is correctly **placed**, but Blender's empty has an exported rotation, so gameplay takes forward from the weapon root's −Z instead of the marker's basis.

Grip to take Wyrmspout v2 from its named collection stand. Hold trigger for sustained fire. Release to cool. Its teeth and spines glow more red as heat rises. A continuous hold reaches overheat in about eight seconds; the weapon stops for three seconds, then resumes automatically if the trigger is still held. Press right B on Knuckles (even if the weapon is held left), or right mouse on desktop, to vent heat while not firing. Left B is reserved for the hand menu. Desktop left mouse fires. The model's eyes follow the player gently within 0.78 m. Fire harms ghosts as actual fire, ignites nearby fuel, and leaves attached burning patches on struck surfaces.

The main tuning constants at the top of `godot/wyrmspout_runtime.gd` are `RANGE` (5.6 m), `FULL_HEAT_SECONDS` (8), `COOL_RATE` (0.18/s), `OVERHEAT_SECONDS` (3), `PATCH_SECONDS` (12), `MAX_PATCHES` (32), `PATCH_INTERVAL` (0.19 s), `PATCH_SPACING` (0.34 m), `STREAM_SPEED` (10.5 m/s), `STREAM_GRAVITY` (5.2 m/s²), `STREAM_LIFETIME` (0.62 s), and `STREAM_RATE` (36 packets/s). Twenty-eight faceted flame meshes are pooled per item. Packets launch at the muzzle over time, fall under gravity, collide with the world, and deal damage along their actual path. Ground strikes feed the same persistent liquid-fire field as Emberdew when available. Other surfaces and enemies receive animated attached flames; one in four patches has a small shadowless light. This is a bounded stylised particle stream and shared ground-fluid simulation, not full volumetric fluid. The model is about 18,280 triangles with roughly 65 nodes; watch headset frame timing if many other detailed assets are added.

The 3D source, four review renders and GLB verifier are in `work/opus-wyrmspout-20260926/output/`; the generated scales texture used by the GLB is in its `input/`. `tools/make_wyrmspout_audio.py` regenerates `godot/assets/wyrmspout-flame.wav`. The old transparent dragon-breath sprite at `godot/assets/wyrmspout-flame.png` is retained as an art source; current fire is procedural faceted geometry. To reinstall the reviewed v2 asset and metadata, stop the managed session, run `node tools/install-wyrmspout-v2.js --dry-run`, then `node tools/install-wyrmspout-v2.js`, then `./start.ps1`. The installer backs up the store and leaves v1 untouched. Do not run the installer while the service is active.

Verify gameplay with `tools/godot/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --xr-mode off --path godot --quit-after 1000 -- --wyrmspout-check`. The check imports the live v2 item, verifies scale/aim/material/audio/eyes/heat surfaces, emits fire, damages a ghost, persists a surface flame, and exercises the three-second overheat. `--wyrmspout-capture=<absolute png path>` opens a short desktop session, waits for Brann's actual delivery, equips the item and saves a rendered firing frame. Runtime logs are in `logs/`, while Godot's own log is also under `user://logs/game.log`.

For a side view of the stream, add `--wyrmspout-side` to that capture command. The output `outputs/wyrmspout-stream-side.png` is a useful reference for packet spacing and gravity. Emissions are timestamped within each frame so a slower render cannot clump multiple packets at the muzzle. The test also confirms a ground strike adds fuel to the shared liquid-fire field.

Lessons from this build:

- PowerShell dropped the empty argument to Claude's `--setting-sources ''`, making the next CLI flag look like its value. The verified subscription runner omits that flag, strips Anthropic/API environment variables, checks the exact personal `claude.ai` Pro identity, and pins `claude-opus-5-5`.
- Direct Godot runs had no imported PNG/WAV resource loader cache. Use `Image.load` plus `ImageTexture.create_from_image`, and parse our simple PCM WAV bytes into `AudioStreamWAV` at runtime. The GLB itself is loaded with `GLTFDocument.append_from_buffer`.
- A rendered screenshot taken as soon as the item is imported can show an invisible weapon because the delivery animation has deliberately hidden it. Wait for `delivery_ready` before visual review. The initial 1,200-frame fallback expired before the queued handoffs finished; even an eight-second delivery timeout failed when v1 Wyrmspout and Siegebird were ahead of v2. The visual check now allows 24 seconds for the serial queue.
- Blender's glTF coordinate check confirmed the muzzle **position** but not the empty's forward basis. The in-game aim test caught the 90-degree marker rotation; use the weapon root's authored −Z for projectiles and effects.

The headset-specific comfort, volume and heating balance still need a real Knuckles playtest. The automated checks establish behavior and import correctness, not the final feel in a player's hand.

Measured timings from `logs/work-timing.csv`: first Opus asset pass 228.81 s, texture/art revision 230.35 s, final ghost/fire integration check 4.02 s, and the successful rendered capture through Brann's serial delivery queue 19.18 s. The managed VR launch after service setup took 0.47 s. The failed early capture attempts and their timings remain in that log so the next visual review starts with the 24-second delivery wait.
