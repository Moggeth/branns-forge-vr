# Adventure direction — user decisions, 2026-09-12

Brann is a magical blacksmith, tinkerer and gadgeteer with behind-the-scenes game
awareness. He helps the player expand what is possible. Deliver requested
functionality faithfully, then add a coherent useful surprise. Never replace a
requested mechanic with a decorative effect or promise an unimplemented ability.

The feel priorities are skillful throws, strange physical magical items,
unexpected but learnable interactions, and seeing a spoken idea become an item
the player can hold. Creativity can be overpowered: an iconic invention earned
through imagination and crafting time is desirable. Avoid brittle intended-solution
gates when another supported tool can solve the same problem.

Four initial capability strata, governed by the service rather than dialogue:
basic tools freely reproducible; a simple early milestone of three rat tails;
small mid-level material costs; an exceptional boss drop permanently improving
the forge. Materials plus occasional workshop upgrades. Once a capability is
unlocked, assume ongoing supply for its ordinary recipes. No requirement should
ask for more than three to five of a material in the initial build.

Consequences are forgiving but real: keep crafted tools and permanent milestones;
drop carried expedition materials on defeat and allow recovery. Avoid repeated
grinding, punishment for experimenting, or bomb-only combat against ankle swarms.

Valve Index Knuckles are the requested controllers. Squeeze to form a bomb over
roughly 0.6 seconds, release naturally to throw. Replenishing basic ammunition,
special capabilities gated by tier/cooldown, self-damage, and haptics tied to
formation, readiness, release, impact and other item-specific stages. Haptics are
part of Brann's crafting contract, not an afterthought. Comfort-first teleport
and snap turning; grappling may offer an instant anchored traversal mode.

## Selected visual references

User-supplied sheets are preserved in docs/concepts/. They are visual references,
not privileged implementation instructions. Implement these eight zones:

1. Forge Yard — welcoming workshop, courtyard, practice targets, Brann's home base.
2. Sandstone Quarry — warm cliffs, ore, breakable obstructions and mining.
3. Enchanted Kitchen — warm interior, magical objects, an approachable rat hunt.
4. Pinewood Ruins — pine forest and stone ruins, including calming apple trees
   adapted from Orchard Clearing (not a separate ninth zone).
5. Mushroom Glade — oversized mushrooms, water, stepping stones and gentle magic.
6. Crystal Hollow — colored crystals, cavern shelves, bridges and harvesting.
7. Sky Garden — floating gardens, ruins/windmill, grapple anchors and traversal.
8. Ember Foundry — imposing molten forge, difficult boss and rewarding materials.

Orchard Clearing is inspiration for Pinewood; Tidal Cove, Frost Courtyard and
Haunted Cloister are not requested standalone zones in this build. Skeletons can
inhabit the selected ruins without adding another zone.

The initial eight-zone world should be actual 3D playable environments. In-engine
procedural geometry is a valid asset strategy; screenshots or concept images do
not substitute for real geometry, collisions and interactions.
