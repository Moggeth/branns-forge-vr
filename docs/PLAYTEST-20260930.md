# FPS/VR playtest integration — 30 September 2026

Launch normally through Brann's Forge Launcher after closing the current game.
The source game loads native code at startup; an existing play session does not
hot-load this pass. No live game, SteamVR process or player save was restarted
or reset during development. Previously active quests remain intact.

## Controls

| Action | FPS mouse and keyboard | VR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move/jump | WASD; Space for a grounded jump | Existing tracked/joystick movement; no new forced jump |
| Pickup/drop/throw | E pickup or gentle drop; X deliberate forward throw | Grip/release with tracked momentum |
| Melee | LMB diagonal upper-right to lower-left strike | Physical swing |
| Orchard's Edge Power Cleave | Hold RMB through LMB contact; glow and READY hint confirm | Hold trigger during physical contact |
| Siegebird | 9 equip; LMB fire; R animated reload; unlimited bolts | Trigger fire; free hand crank and seat belt bolts |
| Siegebird reel | C fits/returns the one real reel within 3 m of its forge supply rack; RMB changes bolt/grapple mode; hold R pulls a live tether; LMB releases it | Fit real reel to the side socket, flip brass selector; free-hand crank reels; holding-hand trigger releases |
| Skull basket | E carry; point at a loose skull within 3.25 m and F stores it; F with no skull targeted retrieves one; E drop | Carry basket; release real skulls into its opening |
| Portal travel | Walk across the opening; F/G destination/travel remain available | Joystick or room-scale walk across the opening; point/trigger remains available |
| Help/fullscreen | F1 full bindings; F11 fullscreen | Existing in-world hints |

Copper Kite retains X for tossing captured cargo. A basket-targeted F action
takes priority over F destination cycling. Installed native weapons use the
explicit mapping in [ITEM-CONTROLS.md](ITEM-CONTROLS.md); generated inventions
keep their installed server/native ability routes, with generic hints rather
than promising an unsupported secondary attack for a plain prop.

## Combat, timber and first job

Siegebird ignores living skeleton interaction boxes and intersects the visible
posed bone triangles instead. Empty gaps do not stop a bolt. Embedded bolts
follow the struck bone; lethal impacts attach to an actual fallen body part.
Ordinary skeleton skull hits are lethal. Torso hits are capped below their
maximum health; shield guardians retain their tougher balance. Scenery and
shield obstruction still precede bone hits.

Three axe strikes in an 18 cm height band fell a tree at that band's cut plane.
The first and second wound sizes differ. The rooted lower mesh remains and the
upper mesh falls; a separate physical log offers intentional E/grip pickup
within 2.15 m, so standing nearby neither collects it nor requires crouching.
Resource inventory/XP is awarded only after that claim succeeds. Scenic trees
use the same cutting and log presentation, with their separate settlement wood
ledger. [Art, saved stump state and felling details](TREE-CUTTING.md).

The first available journal job is **Skulls for the forge**. Accept it near
Brann, take the basket from its forge-approach stand, collect three actual
skeleton heads, then return to claim XP. The basket is portable, persistent,
and permits retrieval. Deposits made before acceptance remain saved and bind
once to the accepted job; duplicate/replayed skull identities cannot award
extra progress. Existing active jobs are not replaced.

Native basket state is `user://skull_basket.json`; queued quest events use the
existing `user://quest_outbox.json`. The local service owns quest rewards and
accepted instance IDs. Do not delete those files to retry a delivery: reconnect
to the service and let the durable pending queue retry.

## Verification and visual review

Standalone diagnostics load no Main scene, service, live saves or XR session:

```powershell
& tools/godot/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --xr-mode off --audio-driver Dummy --path godot --script res://playtest_input_checks.gd
```

The companion standalone scripts are `desktop_locomotion_checks.gd`,
`portal_walkthrough_checks.gd`, `tree_cutting_checks.gd`, `skull_basket_checks.gd`,
`desktop_managed_release_checks.gd`, `item_control_checks.gd`, and
`skeleton_projectile_contact_checks.gd`. Require exit 0, an empty failure array
in their named result, and no SCRIPT ERROR/ERROR text. Managed-release reports
a JSON object with `failures: []`, rather than a bare `RESULT []`.

Actual imported cut/stump/log views can be regenerated with
`--script res://tree_cutting_capture.gd` without `--headless`. Similarly,
`--script res://desktop_swing_capture.gd` produces twelve actual held-model
views (axe, pick and hammer × idle/windup/contact/follow-through) using the
production grip and animation helper. Capture output is ignored under
`outputs/tree-cutting/` and `outputs/desktop-swing/`. On this remote development
PC, add `--position -32000,-32000 --no-focus` to avoid covering the player's
desktop; do not hide the root Window, which prevents GPU rendering.

Final native checks pass; eighteen service tests pass; full disposable service
integration passes in 60.300 s. Snow's native regrid check took 9.664 s and its
actual Mobile-renderer review 18.725 s. Final tree logic took 1.103 s. FPS pose
review took 2.805 s initially and 2.595 s after a small wrist cant kept the axe

rejects distant actors, so this is a helper benchmark, not a claimed live FPS
gain. No individual frame-time or subjective headset acceptance is inferred.

## Limits

Snow is surface coverage over the existing local exposure grid, not volumetric
snow or a whole-world season simulation. Its completed field stays visible
while replacement data scans; falling flakes use fixed world cells recycled
at the local volume boundary.

VR portals infer the torso from the HMD with the same 9.5 cm rearward offset as
the avatar. Crossing must carry the head 40 cm past the opening; short leans
and tracking jumps are rejected. HMD tracking cannot distinguish a deliberate
deep lean from a step. A real headset playtest remains needed for this threshold,
tree falling feel, physical basket reach and final weapon comfort.

Runtime logs are in `logs/`; measured steps are in `logs/work-timing.csv`.
The full integration log is `logs/playtest-integration.log`. No new paid API
call is needed for play; the art commission used Claude subscription sign-in.
