# Ember Foundry authored art — 2026-09-30

Sonnet 5.5 produced a darker basalt-and-iron Foundry kit with a large gate, perimeter rim, broken rail, cooling slag, hanging chains, ember conduits and floor inlays. Codex integrated it into the playable zone. The previous primitive perimeter/gate remains a fallback when the authored manifest is absent. The authoritative lava, floor collision, boss, skeleton, iron ore and travel portal remain at their existing positions.

Actual-game comparison: [before, ground](../assets/ember-foundry-sonnet/previews/before_ground.png), [after, ground](../assets/ember-foundry-sonnet/previews/game_ground.png), [before, RTS](../assets/ember-foundry-sonnet/previews/before_rts.png), [after, RTS](../assets/ember-foundry-sonnet/previews/game_rts.png). Blender previews are in the same `previews/` directory for asset inspection, not as evidence of how the game renders.

The kit has 7 self-contained GLBs, 66 placements, 1,512 unique triangles, 9,756 placed triangles, 19 MultiMesh holders and 31 simple authored collision shapes. Structural, embedded-texture and face-winding validation passed. The focused route check passed all 7 protected samples, with the iron node still present and no new barrier. Under the same whole-world 1200 × 750 review setup, the new ground-eye capture took 340 draw calls, versus the pre-kit 351. This is a comparative sample, not a stereo headset benchmark; VR movement and comfort still need a live playtest.

Source is `assets/ember-foundry-sonnet/build_foundry.py`, editable `foundry.blend`, `collision_intent.json`, Sonnet's `HANDOFF.md` and `stats.json`. Runtime GLBs and manifest are in `godot/assets/zone_art/ember_foundry/`. Rebuild with pinned Blender (`tools/blender-4.5.13-windows-x64/blender.exe -b -P assets/ember-foundry-sonnet/build_foundry.py`), copy the generated GLBs and `placement_ember_foundry.json` to the runtime folder (rename the manifest `placement.json`), then run `scripts/Verify-ZoneArt.ps1 -Zones ember_foundry -Render`. Blender outputs source-directory GLBs during a rebuild; only runtime copies are committed. Godot import sidecars and extracted PNGs are ignored and regenerated.

Limitations: most of the fighting floor is intentionally open, but the broad floor material still reads simply. The gate glow is an emissive plane, and 256px generated textures can look pixelated close up. Future Foundry art can improve the ground material and interactable machinery without narrowing boss movement space. Sonnet's reference images were not consulted after its initial export; Codex compared the actual-game captures during integration.
