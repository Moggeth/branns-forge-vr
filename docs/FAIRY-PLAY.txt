Click the Little Wing window to focus it. F1 / controller Start shows the controls.
WASD / left stick fly. Space/Ctrl / RB/LB rise and fall.
Mouse looks automatically / right stick to look. Shift / L3 boosts. Alt / R3 brakes.
Click / RT fires tiny darts. Right click / LT sends a dust marker.
1-4 / D-pad chooses look, danger, attack or pickup.
Back mouse / E / X carries or drops. Forward mouse / F / A perches. Middle / R / Y reveals.
V / B switches first/third person. C / Back switches hover/acro.
Q/Z rolls in acro; Steam Input rear paddles can emulate these keys.
Escape releases the mouse. Home returns to the host.

AFTER DARK: LITTLE WING & FRIEND
Three new compact night walks: use the AFTER DARK signs at the forge, or J in the fairy window. Fairy darts can activate these signs, so solo practice can travel too. Every space has BACK TO BRANN, NEXT NIGHT WALK and BRIGHTER / MOODY signs.
- Glowroot Grotto: fly near sleepy mushrooms to unfurl their glowing caps. Moths gather around your light.
- Starlace Archive: fly within 2.4m of each of three seals and pulse (Middle mouse / R / Y). A physical gate lifts after all three awaken.
- Moonwater Causeway: nearby stones remember your light for twelve seconds, marking the route for your friend. There is safe footing under the water.
New buddy rituals: fly close to your friend's shoulder and press H (controller: hold D-pad down + A) to perch; press again to leave. A two-second rest on a hand or shoulder leaves a blue light with your friend for twenty seconds after departure. Pulse while settled together to restore 4 fairy health and 6 friend health, once per ten seconds. No revival and no forced camera roll. Solo F3 practice hand also lends light and supports the duet.
Magic includes expanding star rings, shoulder sparks, blooming caps, moth lights and rippling moonwater. Effects use bounded pools; existing reduced-motion feedback setting slows the rings. These discoveries and gate state last for the current session; existing progression saves are unchanged.
