# Sky Garden and Cloudbreak Keep authored environment

Sonnet 5.5 rebuilt the two floating regions as a modular art family. Sky Garden now has a windmill oasis, flowering arches, wind-shaped trees, planted stone paths, waterfalls and layered floating-island cliffs. Cloudbreak Keep uses cooler stone, teal-roofed shelters, banner-lined approaches and distinct grapple landings. The art source is in [`assets/skylands-sonnet/`](../assets/skylands-sonnet/HANDOFF.md), including an editable Blender scene, deterministic builder, 70 GLBs' source definitions and eight studio renders. The installed GLBs and zone-local manifests live in `godot/assets/zone_art/sky_garden/` and `godot/assets/zone_art/cloudbreak_keep/`.

Compare actual-game views: [Sky ground before](../assets/skylands-sonnet/previews/before_sky_ground.png) → [after](../assets/skylands-sonnet/previews/game_sky_ground.png), [Sky RTS before](../assets/skylands-sonnet/previews/before_sky_rts.png) → [after](../assets/skylands-sonnet/previews/game_sky_rts.png); [Cloudbreak ground before](../assets/skylands-sonnet/previews/before_cloud_ground.png) → [after](../assets/skylands-sonnet/previews/game_cloud_ground.png), [Cloudbreak RTS before](../assets/skylands-sonnet/previews/before_cloud_rts.png) → [after](../assets/skylands-sonnet/previews/game_cloud_rts.png). The studio renders communicate asset intent, but these Godot captures are the integration evidence.

The commission used the verified personal Claude.ai subscription and reported `claude-sonnet-5-5`, finishing in 1,031.562 seconds. Its manifests place 222 meshes in Sky Garden and 262 in Cloudbreak Keep; the shared zone-kit adapter batches them into 103 and 97 render holders respectively. The old rectangular tops remain authoritative collision floors, while their crude gray sides/cones, primitive shelters and Sky Garden's old cloud-rock backdrop are retired when an authored manifest is present. Seven grapple anchors, six sky-bloom pickups, both portal pairs and the windmill control remain intact. The authored windmill rotor is separately instantiated under the original animated rotor node; its static manifest placement is removed from the installed Sky Garden manifest. The authored grapple rings replace the old primitive visuals, while the original target bodies stay active.

The integration adds 40 selective collision shapes in Sky Garden and 58 in Cloudbreak Keep through `assets/skylands-sonnet/skl_collision.py`. Thin tree-trunk, tower, post, wall and shelter proxies make major visible structures solid without building collision from art meshes or closing grapple lanes. The builder applies the same collision intent on regeneration; the installed manifests carry it into the game. The focused check now probes tower collisions and confirms arrival routes stay clear.

The two zones contain approximately 127k and 98k placed triangles including distant islets; 12 and 11 material families, respectively. The source GLBs embed their texture maps, so Godot currently extracts some repeated textures per asset. This is a candidate for future import-time material deduplication if stereo profiling shows texture residency or loading cost is material. In native desktop mono captures on the RTX 3080 Ti, both zones hit the game's 120 FPS cap: Sky Garden reported 229 visible objects and 217 draw calls; Cloudbreak Keep reported 216 and 204. These numbers establish a desktop composition and rough rendering budget, **not** a headset stereo guarantee.

Verification: Godot imported all 72 zone-local GLB references in 19.591 seconds. The focused `skylands_art_checks.gd` verifies all island floor colliders, major authored solids, resources, grapple targets, independently animated rotor and unobstructed authored arrival routes. All 22 portal capsule/ray checks also passed after adding the selective proxies. The final 24-view actual-game environmental review passed with no capture failures in 12.016 seconds. Native capture of both zones took 24.238 seconds, and the full disposable-fixture integration suite passed in 59.101 seconds before the collision supplement. The small follow-up removal of old cloud rocks passed the focused check and environmental review. Headset-scale judgement and two-eye frame timing remain playtest work.

Run the focused check with `tools/godot/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --xr-mode off --audio-driver Dummy --path godot --script res://skylands_art_checks.gd --quit-after 2400`. Run the game-view capture with `--xr-mode off --audio-driver Dummy --path godot --script res://environment_art_review.gd --quit-after 2400 -- --world-builder-preview`. Both regions fall back to their old procedural dressing if the installed manifests are absent.

The first PowerShell installation command tried `Copy-Item -LiteralPath` with `previews/*` and `textures/*`. `-LiteralPath` treats the asterisk literally, so the preview/texture copy failed while the 72 GLBs copied correctly. The fast path is to enumerate exact files with `Get-ChildItem -LiteralPath <directory> -File` and copy each `FullName`; the repair took 0.122 seconds. The first physics floor-ray check also stopped at a windmill/harvestable collider. The test now excludes successive non-floor RIDs to verify the actual standing floor beneath them.
