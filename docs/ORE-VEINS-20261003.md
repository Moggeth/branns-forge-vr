# Mountain-foot ore veins — 2026-10-03

Mitch asked for natural, mineable ore along the mountainous edge of the Orchard that respawns the way
World of Warcraft nodes do. His choices: both the existing ores and new ones (groundwork for more
craftables), tiered rarity, a mined node respawns a few minutes later somewhere else, nodes are shared
(first pick to break one gets the ore), and a subtle glint so they can be spotted from afar.

## Rules (`multiplayer/shared/ore-veins.js`)

- 12 vein slots spread evenly around the rim. Each holds at most one vein, so there are never more
  than 12 and the world never clutters. A fresh world (or an older save) fills every slot on its
  first tick.
- Veins sit in a band from the meadow edge (Chebyshev distance 38.5) up the mountain foot to the
  highest ground a Hero can walk to (47.4). Placement rejects steep ground, trees, landmarks,
  bridges, structures and other deposits, and the test walks a Hero to every vein with the real
  terrain mover.
- Rarity by depth into the band. Measured share: copper 38%, iron 38%, gold 12%, mithril 7%, and
  star 5%, which appears only in the deepest third.
- Mining is unchanged: the existing pick rules and rewards. When a vein is mined out, a 3–5 minute
  timer starts. The replacement then rises at least 14 m away within the same stretch of rim,
  never within 10 m of a Hero.
- Veins are ordinary `deposits` with two optional fields: `vein` (the slot) and `respawnTick` (only
  on a spent vein). Saves validate both. Spent veins keep their timer through deposit
  reclamation. The shared 80-deposit budget still applies.

## New materials (`multiplayer/shared/material-catalog.js`)

| Deposit | Drops | Refines into |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copper lode (`copper`) | 4 copper ore | Copper (100 ticks) |
| Gold seam (`gold`) | 3 gold ore | Gold (160 ticks) |
| Star vein (`star`) | 3 star ore | Star metal (existing) |

Iron and mithril veins reuse the existing seams. Copper and Gold are stored, carried, deposited
and refined like every other material. Their catalog `recipes` lists are empty and flagged
`groundwork: true` until their first craftables are designed; new recipes list themselves there. New keys append to `MATERIAL_KEYS`, so older inventories restore
as zero.

## Presentation

- `client/forest-resource-art.js` gains three sculpted deposit kits:
  - copper: green-patina lode with native copper nodules
  - gold: dark host rock cut by a white quartz seam with gold nuggets
  - star: dark geode with amber star crystals
- It also adds copper and gold ore and ingot pickups. Deposit sculpts are now chosen by style
  (nodule, seam, geode), and the existing iron, mithril and crystal kits are unchanged.
- `client/ore-glint.js` draws one additive Points layer. Each live vein gets a soft breathing glow
  in its ore colour plus star sparkles that flash in turn. A minimum pixel size keeps it findable
  across the map, and it fades back close up so the rock reads.
- The Stores tab, quick build names, VR panel and ore grabbing all derive from the catalog.

## Verification

```
node multiplayer/tests/ore-veins.mjs
```

The test covers placement, reachability, tiers, the respawn timer and its survival through
save/load and reclamation, the hero clearance, the deposit budget, legacy saves, malformed save
fields and determinism. `tests/ore-veins-art.html` is a review page for the kits and the glint.
In-game captures used crafted saves next to veins in solo, rendered with SwiftShader. Phone and
headset frame timing is unverified.
