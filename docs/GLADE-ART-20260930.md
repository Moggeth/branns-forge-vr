# Mushroom Glade authored art — 2026-09-30

Sonnet 5.5 created the new violet mushroom family, rooted clusters, mossy mineral backdrop, broken ruin, waterfall and textured pond/bank. Codex integrated that static art while retaining the wisp, all five spore pickups, travel portals and a safe-to-wade-through floor. The earlier disc, generic mushrooms, wall slabs and waterfall sheet remain a fallback when the authored manifest is absent.

Compare [the previous ground view](../assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/previews/before_glade_ground.png) with the [actual-game ground view](../assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/previews/game_ground.png), and [the previous RTS view](../assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/previews/before_glade_rts.png) with the [actual-game RTS view](../assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/previews/game_rts.png). Sonnet's [ground](../assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/previews/preview_glade_ground.png) and [elevated](../assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/previews/preview_glade_rts.png) previews are asset references; gameplay acceptance uses the Godot captures.

The editable source, original placement manifest, textures, fourteen individual GLBs, Blend and handoff are in `assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/`. `godot/assets/zone_art/mushroom_glade/` holds the installed GLBs and prepared placement manifest. `prepare_runtime_manifest.py` flattens the source GLB paths, gives major mushrooms and boulders conservative stem/body collision plus a rear cliff collider, and shrinks bright decorative spore clouds so the world remains legible at eye height. It does not alter the five pickup entities. The author handoff says 70 placements, but its final source manifest contains **59** after keepout fixes; the installed kit uses that validated final file.

The installed kit has 14 GLBs, 59 placements, 10,437 unique triangles, 63 MultiMesh holders and 25 simple collision shapes. Structural, embedded-texture, UV and face-winding validation passed. A clean rebuild produced all 14 GLBs with byte-identical hashes; the source prints `GLADE_BUILD_OK` on success. The native route check passed all ten protected positions with five spore resources and no new barrier. In the whole-world 1200 × 750 capture, the Glade measured **320 ground-eye and 471 RTS draw calls**. This is a desktop render comparison, not a headset frame-time benchmark.

Rebuild with pinned Blender, then prepare and verify:

```powershell
& .\tools\blender-4.5.13-windows-x64\blender.exe -b -P assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/build_glade.py -- work/glade-rebuild
Copy-Item work/glade-rebuild/glb/*.glb assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/source-glbs/
Copy-Item work/glade-rebuild/glb/*.glb godot/assets/zone_art/mushroom_glade/
Copy-Item work/glade-rebuild/placement_mushroom_glade.json assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/
Copy-Item work/glade-rebuild/stats.json assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/
python assets/mushroom-glade-sonnet/prepare_runtime_manifest.py
& .\scripts\Verify-ZoneArt.ps1 -Zones mushroom_glade -Render
```

The water and waterfall remain static textured geometry; UV scrolling and wet splash/audio are future work. The large mushroom caps are intentionally non-solid while their stems collide. Live VR scale, route comfort and night-time glow still need a headset pass. Relaunch the game to see this art; the existing process does not hot-load the files.
