# Forge Yard practice targets and Sunfall Sledge

Four physical pumpkins live near the Forge Yard practice area. Their stable body receives swept VR melee hits, desktop strikes, thrown tools, and Siegebird bolts. A gentle nudge moves one without destroying it. Pointed hits carve eyes and then a glowing mouth over three strikes; subsequent stabs retain the completed face. A blade slices a pumpkin into two colliding rind-and-flesh halves along the blade's wrist roll, while a blunt hit makes eight physical chunks. Broken targets return after 50 seconds; fragments expire after 32 seconds. These are session targets, not world-save inventory.

The current contact category is an item's `impact_class` metadata when present (`stab`, `slice`, or `blunt`), then its ID/display name (axes and blades slice; picks, spears and bolts stab), then blunt. Future generated tools should specify `impact_class` rather than relying on a name. The authored geometry stays simple; contact orientation supplies the varied result.

Sunfall Sledge's native contact is in `godot/sunfall_runtime.gd`. Its impact works on enemies, pumpkins, loose rigid props and solid ground. Eight deterministic boom-bonk samples rotate through four 3D audio voices. Ground contact emits a short radial wave of twelve fiery earth spikes. A direct skeleton impact first disarms any guardian shield, then deals a lethal blow with a stronger physical-bone scatter impulse. Its existing trigger Sunburst remains available. When tuning feel, start with `IMPACT_COOLDOWN`, `SHOCKWAVE_SECONDS` and the impulse in `impact()`; keep a one-hit skeleton fixture.

Verification: `tools/godot/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --xr-mode off --path godot --script res://pumpkin_sunfall_checks.gd -- --smoke` prints `PUMPKIN_SUNFALL_RESULT`. A desktop scene render uses `--xr-mode off --audio-driver Dummy --path godot -- --capture=<absolute png> --capture-pumpkins` without `--headless`. The preview changes only its own diagnostic scene and does not write the player's save. A headset playtest is still needed for exact strike thresholds, hand pose and the audio mix.
