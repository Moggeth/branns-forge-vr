# Starfall Ridge environment art

Starfall Ridge now has an authored mining valley: layered perimeter rock strata, contrasting cyan/violet geodes, meteor fragments and scarred impact stone, a worked-stone path, and a ruined observatory beyond the bomb breach. Sonnet 5.5 authored the Blender builder, and Codex repaired its interrupted export, integrated the GLBs, and matched the in-game lighting to the new materials.

Compare actual Godot views: [ground before](../assets/starfall-sonnet/previews/before_ground.png) → [ground after](../assets/starfall-sonnet/previews/game_ground.png), and [RTS before](../assets/starfall-sonnet/previews/before_rts.png) → [RTS after](../assets/starfall-sonnet/previews/game_rts.png). The [source handoff](../assets/starfall-sonnet/HANDOFF.md) gives rebuild commands, collision intent and known limits.

The final kit has 29 GLBs, 294 placements, six material families, 84 batched render holders and 93 simple collider shapes. The existing seven mineable ore deposits, bomb barrier, three grapple anchors, two enemies, return portal and flat authoritative floor remain in place. Old scattered primitive rocks, crystals, path and label retire when the authored manifest is installed. The visible ground skin replaces the old floor box's surface while that box keeps collision. Arrivals, both sides of the breach, the ore, and the route to the observatory remain open. The observatory door is left clear by its physical proxies.

In native desktop mono capture on the RTX 3080 Ti, Starfall reported the 120 FPS game cap, 282 visible objects and 270 draw calls. The final 24-zone-view review completed in 11.24 script seconds without failures. Focused Starfall and 22-portal checks passed, as did the full disposable-fixture integration suite in 61.831 seconds. A clean isolated rebuild of the canonical source produced all 29 GLBs and collision intent in 3.485 seconds. Godot imported the GLBs in 14.784 seconds. These are desktop evidence, **not** a VR stereo timing guarantee. The ground's small crater depression and outer micro-relief are visual only; those portions need a terrain-collider integration if they become playable routes.

The Claude commission used the verified personal Claude.ai subscription; the JSONL session metadata contains `claude-sonnet-5-5`. It hit the subscription session limit after 581.481 seconds with a 73 KB builder but before execution, previews or a handoff. Codex ran and repaired the builder in the isolated output folder. Three failures were discriminating: a stale null object in Blender's view layer broke GLB export, moving color-space assignment after pixel upload made every texture black, and ground-quad winding hid the surface. The repaired builder rendered the full preview set in 5.0 seconds. Future art briefs should require a first runnable export early, with an explicit image nonblack check and a render of both ground and overhead before spending the full session on refinements.
